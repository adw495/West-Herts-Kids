Watford FC's Community Sports & Education Trust runs **school-holiday football camps for boys and girls aged 6–13** at Westfield Academy on Tolpits Lane, 9am–4pm on the school's 3G AstroTurf pitch. The last camp was October half term 2026; when we checked on 2 November the trust hadn't yet published dates for its next Watford camp, so check its football camps page or booking list.

The trust says sessions are aimed at children who are keen on football and want to improve. Coaching covers skills such as 1v1s, dribbling, passing and finishing, plus small-sided games. Its coaches are FA qualified, DBS checked and first aid trained.

At the October 2026 camp places cost **£30 a day**, with 10% off if you book every day of the week. Children need AstroTurf trainers or moulded boots, shin pads, a water bottle, waterproofs, sun cream and a packed lunch, plus indoor shoes in case of very bad weather. The trust isn't Ofsted registered, so childcare vouchers can't be used.
