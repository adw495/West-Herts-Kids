Watford FC's Community Sports & Education Trust runs **October half-term football camps for boys and girls aged 6–13** at Westfield Academy on Tolpits Lane. The October 2026 camp runs **Monday 26 to Friday 30 October, 9am–4pm**, on the school's 3G AstroTurf pitch.

The trust says sessions are aimed at children who are keen on football and want to improve. Coaching covers skills such as 1v1s, dribbling, passing and finishing, plus small-sided games. Its coaches are FA qualified, DBS checked and first aid trained.

Places cost **£30 a day**, with 10% off if you book every day of the week. Children need AstroTurf trainers or moulded boots, shin pads, a water bottle, waterproofs, sun cream and a packed lunch, plus indoor shoes in case of very bad weather. The trust isn't Ofsted registered, so childcare vouchers can't be used.
