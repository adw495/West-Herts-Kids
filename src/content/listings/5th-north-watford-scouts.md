5th North Watford meets on the corner of Hamilton Road and Upper Highway in Abbots Langley. **Beavers (6–8)** meet on Mondays, **Cubs (8–10½)** on Tuesdays and **Scouts (10½–14)** on Fridays.

A district Explorer unit for 14–18s meets at the same HQ on Thursdays.

To join, fill in the child interest form on the Watford North Scouts **Join Scouts** page and choose this group.
