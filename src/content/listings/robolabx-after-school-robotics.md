RoboLab X runs weekly **term-time robotics clubs** where children design, build and code projects using motors, LEDs and sensors.

Clubs are for **ages 7 to 11 (Years 3–6)**, need no experience, and run **inside partner schools**, so availability depends on your child's school. Local partner schools named on the provider's site are **Lanchester Primary, The Orchard Primary and Field Junior** in Watford and **Little Green Junior** in Croxley Green.

Most clubs have a school-specific booking link: ask the school office, or email info@robolabx.com for the right sign-up page.
