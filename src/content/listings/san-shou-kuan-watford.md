San Shou Kuan is a kickboxing style built around kicks, punches and takedowns. Its **Little Dragons** class for children aged **5 to 10** runs on **Sundays from 4:30 to 5:15pm** at the Dan Tien Centre in Harebreaks, Watford, which has a free car park at the rear.

Classes run about 50 weeks a year, with short seasonal breaks. You can **try a session for free**. Prices are on the club's website as a table, and include the BCCMA licence and insurance.
