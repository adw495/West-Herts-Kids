**Tiny Tots** is the toddler group at All Saints Church in Kings Langley. It runs **every Wednesday in term time, 10am to 11.30am**, in the church, for little ones aged 0–3 and their parents or carers (the parish site describes it as for pre-school children).

Expect toys and craft, songs and stories, with refreshments and a chat for the grown-ups. The church says all are welcome.

No price is published, so ask on your first visit. The church's contact number is 07503 950315.
