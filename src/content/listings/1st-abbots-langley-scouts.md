3rd North Watford, also known as **1st Abbots Langley**, meets at its HQ on Langley Road.

It runs two Beaver colonies for 6–8s (**Tuesdays** and **Saturdays**), two Cub packs for 8–10½s (**Mondays** and **Wednesdays**) and a Scout troop for 10½–14s on **Thursdays**. A district Explorer unit for 14–18s also meets at the HQ on Tuesdays.

To join, fill in the child interest form on the Watford North Scouts **Join Scouts** page and choose this group.
