Let's Play runs school-holiday Fun Days at Watford Woodside Leisure Centre for children from **age 5 (Year 1) to 12**. A standard day is 9am to 4pm for £29.50, and there's a half-day option for £12.50.

Early drop-off from 8am and late pick-up until 5pm cost £3.50 each, and siblings get 10% off. Let's Play is Ofsted registered and takes card payments, Tax-Free Childcare and childcare vouchers.
