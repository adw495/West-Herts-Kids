Little Kickers teaches football to pre-schoolers through play, with age-based programmes:

- **18 months to 2½ years**
- **2½ to 3½ years**
- **3½ to 5 years**
- **5 to 8 years** (Mega Kickers, Sunday mornings in Watford)

Locally, there are indoor sessions at **William Penn Leisure Centre** in Rickmansworth and weekend sessions in Watford. Classes are paid by rolling monthly billing, and you can **book a free trial** online.
