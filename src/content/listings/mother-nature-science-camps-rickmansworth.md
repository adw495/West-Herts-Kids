Mother Nature Science Camps runs **science activity holiday camps for ages 5 to 11** at Merchant Taylors' Prep School on Sandy Lodge Road, Moor Park. Each day mixes indoor, hands-on science sessions with outdoor play breaks, and children take home some of their experiments. The February programme covers forces, the human body, engineering challenges, materials and flight, with a parent handout explaining what they did.

Camps run **9am to 3.30pm**, with early drop-off from 8.30am and late pick-up until 4pm (£5 each per day). The Rickmansworth dates so far are **February half term, Monday 15 to Friday 19 February 2027**, and two four-day Easter weeks: **Tuesday 30 March to Friday 2 April** and **Monday 5 to Thursday 8 April 2027**.

A single day costs **£110** and a full week **£345**. There were no Christmas dates at this venue when we checked; its Christmas camps are in London.
