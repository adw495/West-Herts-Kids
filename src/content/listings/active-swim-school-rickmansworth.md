Active Swim School teaches babies, children and adults at three pools around Rickmansworth and Croxley Green: **Rickmansworth School** (25m, Scots Hill, Croxley Green), **Royal Masonic School for Girls** (18m, Rickmansworth Park) and **York House School** (25m, Sarratt Road, Croxley Green, weekends).

Children work through stages following Swim England's Learn to Swim framework, in groups of up to four for beginners and up to six for stronger swimmers. One-to-one and two-to-one lessons are also available, as are **tailored lessons for swimmers with special educational needs**. It runs holiday crash courses too; October half-term 2026 dates were still "to be announced" when we checked.

Prices aren't published online. You can book a free trial or assessment through the website or by phone.
