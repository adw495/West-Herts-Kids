Fudoshin Ju-Jitsu teaches ju-jitsu as practical self-defence rather than a competition sport. Children train in three groups: **infants (5–7)**, **juniors (8–12)** and **intermediates (13–16)**, and the minimum age is 5. Gradings take place two or three times a year.

Locally, classes run at **Queens' School, Bushey** on Wednesdays, **Bushey Grove Leisure Centre** on Thursdays and the **South Oxhey dojo on Gosforth Lane** on Saturday mornings. Children's training is **£40 a month** plus a £40 annual membership, and you can book a **free trial lesson**.
