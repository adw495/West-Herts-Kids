Youngs Football Academy runs **Thursday-evening sessions at Powerleague Watford**, on the 11-a-side astro pitch at Queens' School, Aldenham Road, Bushey. The entrance to the pitch is to the right of the main car park.

The **Development Academy for U7s–U11s (ages 6–11)** runs 6pm–7.15pm and is aimed at entry and intermediate players. The academy says each month focuses on areas such as passing and moving, ball mastery, shooting and fitness, and that it is a pathway into its Youngs FC squads. There are also **goalkeeper training** sessions for U6s–U9s (5.30pm) and U9s–U12s (6.30pm).

The Development Academy costs **£55 a month** by Direct Debit, and new players can book **one free trial session**. Sessions carry on through the year rather than by the term, with breaks over Christmas and some school holidays.
