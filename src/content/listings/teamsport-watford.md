TeamSport Watford is an indoor **electric go-karting** track with a 500m circuit across two levels. **Go-karting is for ages 8 and up**, and first-timers are welcome, with the track team on hand to help.

It also has **Sixes Social Cricket** (a tech-powered batting game) for **ages 5 and up**, a spectator area with food, and hosts parties and events. It's on the same Greatham Road estate as Parents Paradise, about five minutes' drive from Watford town centre.
