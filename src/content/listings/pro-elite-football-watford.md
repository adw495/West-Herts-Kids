Pro Elite Football Academy runs **45-minute weekend football classes** in three age groups: **18 months–3** (getting used to the ball and kicking with confidence), **3–5** and **5–7**. Some classes had waiting lists when we checked.

Weekend venues:

- **Saturday:** St Joan of Arc School, High Street, Rickmansworth (WD3 1HG); Nascot Wood Junior School, Nascot Wood Road, Watford (WD17 4YS)
- **Sunday:** Stanborough Primary School, Appletree Walk, Watford (WD25 0DQ); Thomas Parmiter Sports Centre, High Elms Lane, Watford (WD25 0UU)

The **U6–U8 Development Centre** (ages 5–8) is the next step up: hour-long Monday sessions at 5pm and 6pm at Powerleague Watford, Queens' School, Aldenham Road, Bushey (WD23 2TY), with more ball contact time. Classes are paid monthly, with a **£40 membership fee** on top.
