Hertfordshire Libraries run **free Baby Rhyme Time** sessions: half an hour of songs and rhymes for babies and toddlers with a parent or carer, aimed at early language skills and a chance to meet other local families.

Sessions run every week at **Watford Central, North Watford, Rickmansworth, Croxley Green, Chorleywood, Abbots Langley, Bushey and Oxhey (South Oxhey)** libraries. There's **no booking**, but libraries may cap numbers, so arriving a little early helps. Donations are welcome.

Days and times above are from the libraries' events listing in November 2026. Check the listing for your library before you go, as sessions can pause for holidays or staffing.
