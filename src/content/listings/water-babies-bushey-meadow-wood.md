Water Babies runs weekly baby and toddler swimming lessons at **Meadow Wood School's pool in Bushey**. Lessons are **30 minutes** long, taught through songs, play and repetition, with a parent or carer in the water alongside the teacher.

Classes are grouped by age: **0–1** (baby swimming), **1–2** (toddler), **2–3** and **3–4** (pre-school). Water Babies also runs lessons for children aged 4+ at selected pools, so ask whether this pool offers them.

Days, times and prices depend on your child's age. Use the class finder on the pool page to see what's available.
