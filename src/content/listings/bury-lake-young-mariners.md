Bury Lake Young Mariners is an award-winning youth sailing charity at the **Rickmansworth Aquadrome**, run entirely by volunteers. All its sailing and powerboat courses are RYA-certified.

Children aged **7–9** can try **Junior Days**: short "Messing About in Boats" sessions and a two-day "Oppie Adventures" introduction. From **age 9** (a firm minimum), the **RYA Youth Sailing Courses** run as week-long courses in the school holidays, working through Start Sailing Stages 1 to 4. Autumn half-term courses are bookable on the club's website.

Once children can sail on their own (RYA Stage 2 or above), they can join supervised **General Sailing** on Saturdays and **Splash Club** on Wednesday evenings in summer, with boat, buoyancy aid and safety cover included. Under-12s need a parent or responsible adult on site. After an RYA course, you get three months' free temporary membership. The club is card-only.
