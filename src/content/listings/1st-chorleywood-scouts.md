1st Chorleywood Scouts runs **Beavers (6–8), Cubs (8–10½) and Scouts (10½–14)** from its HQ on Orchard Drive. Cub packs meet on **Thursdays and Fridays**, and Scout troops on **Wednesdays, Thursdays and Fridays**.

Explorer Scouts (14–18) also meet at the HQ, run by Rickmansworth and Chorleywood District.

Places go through the group's **online waiting lists**, linked from its Admissions page. It's worth joining the list early.
