Performance Plus Academy runs **advanced "micro group" football sessions** on Friday evenings at Powerleague Watford, at Queens' School on Aldenham Road, Bushey. There are two groups, **ages 8–10 and ages 10–12**, both 5.30pm to 6.45pm. The academy's other venues are in north-west London.

The groups are billed at **£85 a month**, and new players can book a **trial session for £35**. The winter term runs to Friday 18 December 2026. The booking page gives little detail about the coaching itself, so it's worth asking about level and group size before signing up.
