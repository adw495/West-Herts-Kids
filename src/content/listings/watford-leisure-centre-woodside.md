Watford Woodside (Everyone Active) in Garston has **two pools**, home to its **children's swimming lessons** and swim sessions for all ages and abilities.

There's also a sports hall for table tennis, badminton and five-a-side, a squash court and a **3G artificial football pitch**. It opens 06:00–22:00 on weekdays and 08:00–18:00 at weekends; lesson levels and prices are on the Everyone Active website and app.
