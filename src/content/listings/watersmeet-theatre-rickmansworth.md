Watersmeet is Rickmansworth's theatre and cinema, on the High Street (WD3 1EH). Alongside its adult programme it puts on **family shows and films in the school holidays**. October half term 2026 includes a magic show, a science show, a Disney Junior Cinema Club for preschoolers and family films (*Hook* for £1).

The **Christmas pantomime is Peter Pan, 4–31 December 2026**, with tickets from £13 and concessions for children up to 18. Under-2s go free on an adult's lap. The show runs about 2½ hours with an interval, and Watersmeet says it suits ages 2 and up. There's a **relaxed performance** on Wednesday 16 December at 4.30pm.

For birthdays, Watersmeet offers a **children's party package** (£10 a child, 2–10 guests, plus show tickets): a reserved, decorated table in the theatre bar with drinks, snacks, ice cream and a birthday cake.
