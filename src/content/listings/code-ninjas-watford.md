Code Ninjas Watford has opened at **St Luke's Church, Langley Way** (WD17 3EG). It teaches coding through a game-based curriculum, guided by "Code Senseis":

- **CREATE (ages 8–14):** weekly sessions, working from White to Black belt, starting with block coding and moving on to JavaScript and C#.
- **Holiday camps (ages 5–14):** themed days on topics such as AI, robotics, game design and cybersecurity. October half-term 2026 had camps on 26, 27 and 29 October, 10am–3pm.

You can book a **free session** on the Watford page before signing up. The centre also lists clubs, birthday parties and Parents' Night Out events.
