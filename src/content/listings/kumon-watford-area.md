Kumon runs **individual maths and English programmes**: children attend a study centre and do short daily worksheets at home, averaging about 30 minutes per subject. Kumon teaches from **age two up to pre-university**.

Local centres, each with its own timetable on kumon.co.uk:

- **Croxley Green:** St Oswald's Church Hall, Malvern Way (Tue, Thu, Sat)
- **Chorleywood:** Christ Church, The Common (Tue, Wed, Sat)
- **Bushey:** Sacred Heart Parish Centre, London Road (Mon), and The Grange Academy (Sat)
- **Watford:** St Luke's Church, Langley Way (Mon, Thu)
- **Watford Vicarage Road:** West Watford Community Association, Harwoods Road (Tue, Thu, Sat)
- **Garston:** Meriden Community Centre, Garsmouth Way (Tue, Wed, Sat)
- **Carpenders Park:** All Saints Church, Gosforth Lane (Tue, Fri, Sat)

Each centre is run independently and sets its own fees.
