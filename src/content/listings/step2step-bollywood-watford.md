Sumeet's Step2Step Bollywood Dance Academy runs a **Sunday class for pre-teens (ages 7–12)** in Watford, 11.15am to 12.15pm. Each class starts with a warm-up and stretch, then works through dance styles including Bollywood, Bhangra, folk, semi-classical, modern jazz, hip hop and contemporary.

Classes are held on the first floor of the **FullerLife Health and Fitness Centre** at Watford Grammar School for Girls. Go in through the FullerLife main gate, not the school entrance.

The academy says all students get the chance to perform in a summer and a winter show each year. You can book a **£13 trial class** before paying for the term. The Watford class is for 7–12s; the 13+ class at the same venue is an adult class.
