Livelykicks runs **football development groups** for children of all abilities, split into **ages 3–5, 6–8 and 9–11**. Sessions focus on basic skills and enjoying the game, and players who want more can move on to the club's teams for friendly matches and tournaments.

In **Bushey**, sessions are at **Metropolitan Bushey**, Aldenham Road (WD23 2TR), on **Monday afternoons** and **Saturday mornings**. In **Watford**, a Saturday morning session for 3–5s runs at **Westfield Academy**, Tolpits Lane (WD18 6NS). Parking at Metropolitan Bushey is pay-on-exit.

New players can try **two sessions free**. Livelykicks also runs holiday camps, Sunday and Saturday teams, after-school clubs and football parties; book through its ClassForKids page.
