1st North Watford meets on Leggatts Way. **Beavers (6–8)** meet on Thursdays, **Cubs (8–10½)** on Mondays and **Scouts (10½–14)** on Thursdays.

Older young people (14–18) can carry on through the district's Explorer units.

To join, fill in the child interest form on the Watford North Scouts **Join Scouts** page and choose this group.
