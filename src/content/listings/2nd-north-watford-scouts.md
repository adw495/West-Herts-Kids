2nd North Watford meets at its HQ on The Harebreaks. **Beavers (6–8)** meet on Fridays, **Cubs (8–10½)** on Thursdays and **Scouts (10½–14)** on Mondays.

A district Explorer unit for 14–18s uses the same HQ on Wednesdays.

To join, fill in the child interest form on the Watford North Scouts **Join Scouts** page and choose this group.
