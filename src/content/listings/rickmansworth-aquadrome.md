The Aquadrome is a **local nature reserve** run by Three Rivers District Council, with lakes, woodland and paths along the River Colne and the Grand Union Canal.

It's a good free option for a family walk, a scoot or bike ride, or spotting wildlife. Paths can get muddy after rain, so wellies are a good idea.

**Works from 12 October 2026:** the council is carrying out asbestos remediation in the picnic area and wet woodland, and some car park bays will be closed while the work goes on. There's also a blue-green algae alert on Batchworth Lake, so keep dogs out of the water. Check the [council's Aquadrome page](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/rickmansworth-aquadrome) for the latest before you go.
