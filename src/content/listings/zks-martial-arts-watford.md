ZKS Martial Arts teaches children in Watford at **Nascot Wood Junior School**. Classes are grouped by age: **Little Dragons (3–4)**, **Mini Ninjas (5–7)**, **Junior MA (7–11)** and **Teen MA (11–16)**, covering karate, kickboxing, judo and self-defence skills.

Watford classes run on **Monday, Wednesday and Friday evenings and Saturday mornings**. Membership is monthly by Direct Debit, with a **free trial** first and a one-off joining fee if you carry on.
