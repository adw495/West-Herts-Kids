Perform's curriculum is based on **"The 4 Cs": Confidence, Concentration, Communication and Coordination**, taught through drama, dance and singing.

There are classes for **4–7s and 7–12s** at the **Watford Quaker Meeting House (Fridays)** and **The Henderson Hub in Abbots Langley (Thursdays)**. Perform also runs holiday courses; check its website for the nearest venue. Every child can have a **free trial session** first.
