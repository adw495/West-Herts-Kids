Bushey Museum & Art Gallery tells the story of Bushey and its unusual artistic history, including the Herkomer art school, with changing exhibitions through the year. It was named a VisitEngland "Hidden Gem" in 2026.

**Entry is free** (donations welcome) and it opens **Thursday to Sunday, 11am–4pm**. There's paid parking in Kemp Place car park opposite the Red Lion. It's a small, quiet museum, so it suits a short visit or a rainy-day stop rather than a whole day out; check the current exhibitions before you go.
