FJ Sporting Solutions runs school-holiday camps at **Langleybury Cricket Club** in Hunton Bridge, with on-site parking. Its May half-term camp mixed cricket, football, athletics, team games and arts and crafts for ages 3 to 15.

This October half term (Wednesday 28 and Thursday 29 October 2026) it's running a **LEGO Camp for ages 4 to 16**, 9am to 3pm, with building challenges, iPad activities, inflatables and outdoor play. It costs £30 a day, with early drop-off from 8am and late pick-up until 4pm at £5 each.
