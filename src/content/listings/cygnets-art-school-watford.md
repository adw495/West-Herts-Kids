Cygnets Art School's Watford branch teaches traditional fine art skills (drawing, painting and sculpture) at **Beechen Grove Baptist Church** on Clarendon Road. Each term follows a new artistic theme.

- **All-abilities classes (ages 5–12):** Mondays 4–5pm and Saturdays 11.15am–12.15pm, **£49.50 a month**, trial lesson £18
- **Intermediate classes (ages 9–13):** Mondays 5–6.30pm and Saturdays 1–2.30pm, **£66 a month**, trial lesson £24. These are a little more advanced, for children who want a more serious approach to art

It also runs one-off **workshops every half-term and school holiday**, where children complete a full art project in a single session. The October 2026 workshops run from Tuesday 27 to Thursday 29 October.
