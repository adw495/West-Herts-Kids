Shogun Practical Karate runs children's karate classes at several local venues: **Arnett Hills School in Rickmansworth** on Mondays, **All Saints Church, Gosforth Lane, South Oxhey** on Tuesdays and Thursdays, **Bromet School, Oxhey Road, Watford** on Wednesdays and **The Grange Academy, London Road, Bushey** on Saturday mornings.

Classes are split by age, usually **5–10** and **11–17**, and Bushey also has a Saturday family class. You can book a **free trial class** online. The club doesn't publish prices on its website.
