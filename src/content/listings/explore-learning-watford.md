Explore Learning runs an **Ofsted-registered tuition centre inside the Harlequin shopping centre** in Watford, for children aged **4 to 16**. It covers maths and English, SATs, 11+ and entrance exams, and GCSE maths. Group sessions have **up to six children per tutor**, each working on their own personalised work; one-to-one sessions are also offered.

The centre is open **Tuesday to Saturday**: 11am–8pm on Tuesdays and Wednesdays, 11am–7pm on Thursdays, 9am–6pm on Fridays and 9am–5pm on Saturdays.

Membership is monthly, with **no joining fee** and you can cancel at any time. Group maths and English starts at **£139 a month** for one session a week. You can book a **free trial session** first. Online tuition is also available for families who prefer it.
