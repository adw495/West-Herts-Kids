Charlotte Amelia School of Dance runs evening and Saturday classes at **Cassiobury Junior School** in west Watford:

- **Ballet**, including Ballet with Mum/Dad for 18 months to 3, Pre Primary and Primary Ballet, and RAD graded classes
- **Jazz** and **lyrical** classes from age 4
- **Acrobatics** from beginners (4–7) up to 16
- **Competition classes** for 6–9s and 10–13s

Classes are booked by the term through the school's ClassForKids page, and you can **try a class free** first. It also runs after-school dance clubs at Cassiobury Infants and Bushey Heath Primary.
