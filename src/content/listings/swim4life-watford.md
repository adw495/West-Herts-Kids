Swim4Life Watford is a family-run swim school that opened in January 2022. It teaches small classes for children aged one and over, in 30 and 45-minute lessons that follow the Swim England stages, and most of its teachers are also school teachers.

Lessons run at two pools in central Watford: Watford Grammar School for Girls (reached through the blue gate on Vicarage Road) and Cassio Metro Leisure Centre on Linden Avenue. The 2026 schedule includes a Parent + Toddler class on Saturdays from 11:30am to 12:30pm at Watford Girls. Prices aren't published on the website; you register interest through its booking page.
