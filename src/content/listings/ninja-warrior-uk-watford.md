Ninja Warrior UK Watford is an indoor adventure park at Woodside Leisure Park with **fixed ninja runs and an inflatable course**. Main sessions are for **ages 5 to adult**. Under-8s must be accompanied on the activities, under-12s need a parent or guardian on site, and most of the fixed ninja runs need climbers to be at least 140cm tall.

**Ninja Toddlers** is for 1–4 year olds on Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday mornings (9.30–11.30am) in term time. Each adult can bring up to two toddlers and supervises them throughout. Everyone needs the venue's safety socks.

Weekday afternoons from 4pm, weekends and school holidays are peak times. Parties are available and parking is free.
