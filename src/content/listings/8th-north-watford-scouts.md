8th North Watford meets at All Saints Church on All Saints Crescent. **Beavers (6–8)** and **Cubs (8–10½)** meet on Wednesdays, and **Scouts (10½–14)** on Thursdays.

The district notes that the group isn't affiliated with any religion, even though it meets in a church hall.

To join, fill in the child interest form on the Watford North Scouts **Join Scouts** page and choose this group.
