Rugbytots runs rugby-based play sessions in three age groups: **2–3½** (30 minutes), **3½–5** and **5–7** (45 minutes each).

Locally, classes run on **Saturday mornings** at Parmiters School, High Elms Lane, Watford (WD25 0JU) and St Margaret's Sports Centre, Merry Hill Road, Bushey (WD23 1DT), and on **Sunday mornings** at Harvey Road Primary School, Croxley Green (WD3 3BN), which Rugbytots lists as "Rickmansworth Sundays".

Bookings are termly through the Rugbytots website, prorated if you join mid-term. **Free tasters** are offered when there's space. Some Croxley Green classes had waiting lists when we checked.
