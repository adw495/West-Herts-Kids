Chorleywood Lawn Tennis Club's coaching programme is run by **BR Tennis** for **children aged 3 to 18**. Tots Tennis starts at 3, then children move through **Red, Orange, Green and Yellow ball** groups, with 10U/11U squads, matchplay and a Teen Tennis group for 13–18s.

Groups run as **term-long courses** on most days of the week, mainly after school on weekdays and on weekend mornings. Non-members can book at a higher price than members. Everything is booked online through the club's ClubSpark coaching page, which shows which groups are full.
