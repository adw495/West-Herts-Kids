Chorleywood Lawn Tennis Club coaches **children aged 4 to 18**. **Mini Tennis** (Red, Orange and Green) for 4–10 year olds runs at up to eight children per coach. From 11, juniors play on full-size courts in development, club and performance groups.

Children join the club to take part, and new families can **try it for a month** first. Sessions are booked through the TeamUp app. Times and prices aren't published online, so check with the coaching team.
