Rickmansworth Sailing Club is a volunteer-run **RYA Training Centre** on Troy Lake in West Hyde. The club sails all year round, and runs training for juniors as well as adults using the RYA Youth Sailing Scheme. A stage usually takes two days.

For younger children, **Trojans** sessions (ages **4–8**) run on set Saturdays from 11am to 4:30pm, getting children out on the water in a boat with an adult, with a lunch break and free sailing with safety cover. They're mainly for members, but the club says it may be able to welcome interested youngsters. The club is also part of the **RYA OnBoard** programme for 8–18 year olds.

Junior members must be under 18 and have a named adult member responsible for them. Training dates depend on volunteer instructors, so contact the club to ask about upcoming courses and tell them your child's age.
