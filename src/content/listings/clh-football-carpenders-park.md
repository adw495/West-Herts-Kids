CLH Football runs weekly football sessions at **St Meryl School** in Carpenders Park, next to South Oxhey, grouped by school year from **Reception to Year 7**.

- **Wednesdays:** Reception–Year 3 at 4.30pm, Years 4–7 at 5.30pm
- **Saturdays:** Reception and Years 1–3 at 9am, Years 4–5 at 10am, Years 6–7 at 11am

Sessions run through the school year and are paid by **monthly subscription (£55 a month)**. New players can book a **free trial session** first through CLH's ClassForKids page.
