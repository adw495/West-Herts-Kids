Croxley Green Baptist Church on Baldwins Lane runs both a **Girls' Brigade** and a **Boys' Brigade**, which are long-running national youth organisations.

**Girls' Brigade** is for girls from **Reception upwards** and meets on **Monday evenings, 6–7.15pm**, in term time. The church describes it as a mix of arts, crafts and games, with a Christian ethos. **Boys' Brigade** is for boys in **School Years 4 to 6** and meets on **Wednesday evenings, 6.15–7.45pm**, in term time, with a range of fun activities.

Term dates are on the church's online calendar. You can ask questions through the church office by email.
