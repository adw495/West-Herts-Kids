Jo Jingles runs music and movement classes for babies and young children, built around the Early Years Foundation Stage. The **Watford Area** franchise runs classes in age groups: **Baby Jingles** (from about 5 months to walking), **Jingle Toddlers** (walking to 2), **Jo Jinglers** (over 2s), **Jingle Juniors** (3–5) and mixed-age **Family Time** (walking to 4).

In Watford, classes run on **Wednesday mornings at the YMCA Orbital Community Hub**, Haines Way (WD25 7QU), and on **Friday mornings at The Dan Tien Performing Arts School**, The Square (WD24 6NJ). In Bushey, there are **Tuesday and Wednesday** classes at **Parents Paradise** soft play, Greatham Road (WD23 2NZ), where the provider says you get a free hour of soft play before the class.

Classes cost **£8 each** and are booked by the term through the Jo Jingles booking site. Shoes aren't worn in the Dan Tien dance studio, so bring socks.
