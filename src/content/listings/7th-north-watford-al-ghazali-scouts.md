7th North Watford (Al Ghazali) is a **Muslim-led** Scout group that meets at Beechfield School on Gammons Lane.

**Beavers (6–8), Cubs (8–10½) and Scouts (10½–14)** all meet **every other Tuesday**, with separate girls' and boys' Scout sections.

To join, fill in the child interest form on the Watford North Scouts **Join Scouts** page and choose this group.
