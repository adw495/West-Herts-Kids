Watford Gracie Jiu-Jitsu Academy teaches its **Gracie BullySafe** programme to children in three age groups: **Tiny Titans (ages 3+)**, **Little Grapplers (5–7)** and **Junior Grapplers (8–12)**. The academy says children learn verbal assertiveness and non-violent, leverage-based techniques, with no punching or kicking.

Children's classes run on **Monday, Wednesday and Friday after school and at weekends**. Membership is **£43 a month for one class a week or £58 for two**, and Tiny Titans is £35 a month. You can start with a **10-day free trial**, with a uniform provided.
