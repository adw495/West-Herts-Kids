Watford Gymnastics Club is a family-run British Gymnastics club at **Watford Business Park**, with a foam pit. For under-5s there's a **parent-and-toddler soft play** drop-in on Tuesday mornings in term time, **Leaping Lions** (from 2½, with a grown-up) and **Tumbling Tigers** (3–4, coach-led).

Weekly **general gymnastics** classes are grouped by school year, from **Reception up to 16**, on weekday evenings and weekend mornings, working through the British Gymnastics badge awards. The club also runs Teen Gym, adult and **SEND sessions**.

**Holiday sessions** are open to members and non-members from Reception up. The club suggests booking a paid trial in a class with space, which then becomes your child's weekly slot.
