Hertfordshire Libraries' **LEGO clubs** set fun building challenges designed to develop problem solving, spatial awareness and creative thinking. They're for **children aged 5 to 11** (5 to 12 at Bushey); under-5s can't attend, and **under-8s must be accompanied by an adult**.

Weekly clubs run at **Watford Central** (Monday after school), **Bushey** (Wednesday and Friday after school), **Oxhey** in South Oxhey (Saturday morning) and **Abbots Langley** (Saturday morning). Abbots Langley also runs a **Quiet LEGO** session before the main club for children who find a busy library hard.

Sessions cost **£4.40 per child**. Places are limited, so book at the library where asked. Times above are from the libraries' events listing in November 2026.
