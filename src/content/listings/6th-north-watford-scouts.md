6th North Watford runs **Beavers (6–8) on Wednesday evenings**, **Cubs (8–10) on Thursday evenings** and **Scouts (10–14) on Monday evenings**, at the bottom of East Drive by the children's play area.

Children can **come along for a few weeks** before joining, with no payment or uniform needed. After that, membership is **£40 a term**, and the group says help is available if cost is an issue.

Enquire through the contact form on the group's website. Places follow its published waiting-list policy.
