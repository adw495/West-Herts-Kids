Jigsaw Arts Watford is a weekend performing arts school for **ages 3 to 18**, with classes on **Saturday mornings at Watford Grammar School for Girls** (Lady's Close, WD18 0AE) and **Saturday afternoons at Watford Grammar School for Boys** (Rickmansworth Road, WD18 7JF). Its page also mentions Sunday mornings at The Grange Academy in Bushey.

- **Early Years (3–4 and 5–6):** 90-minute sessions split equally between singing, dance and drama, with one teacher
- **Main School (7–18):** 3-hour sessions with separate specialist teachers for drama, dance and singing

You can book a **two-week trial**. The Autumn 2026 term runs from 19 September to 28 November. Jigsaw also runs holiday camps; the October half-term camp is on **Tuesday 27 October**.
