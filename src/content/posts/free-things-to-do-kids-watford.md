Keeping the kids busy doesn't have to cost a fortune, and West Herts has plenty of big parks, ancient woods, lakes and canal towpaths that are free to explore all year. We've checked every spot below against the council's, venue's or organiser's own website, so you can head out knowing what to expect.

## Parks and playgrounds

- **Cassiobury Park**, Watford. Watford's 250-acre Green Flag park has two children's playgrounds, cafés, a bandstand and woodland and riverside walks. The Watford Miniature Railway runs here too, at a small cost per ride. The council's Cassiobury Park car park on Gade Avenue is free for up to 2 hours, then charged (from £3.10 for up to 3 hours, maximum stay 6 hours). [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/345/cassiobury-park-and-whippendell-wood) · [Parking](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/25/cassiobury-park-car-park) · [Our listing](/activities/cassiobury-park/) · [Miniature railway](/activities/watford-miniature-railway/)
- **Oxhey Activity Park**, Wiggenhall Road, Watford. A modern park with a skate park for skating, BMX and scooting, a bike pump track, a playground with play trails, a jungle swing and slides designed to be accessible for disabled children, plus free outdoor table tennis (bring your own bats and ball) and a riverside café. It's free to use and open daily; car parking is limited. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/oxhey-activity-park/oxhey-activity-park-1)
- **Oxhey Park**, Eastbury Road, Watford. A Green Flag park with a children's playground, a mini football pitch (no booking needed), an outdoor gym, woodland and the River Colne. Parking is on the street. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/373/oxhey-park)
- **Leavesden Country Park**, College Road, Abbots Langley. A play area, woodland walks, the Woodlands Café and two self-guided trails (the Heritage Sculpture Trail and the East Lane Cemetery Walk) that are suitable for buggies. There are toilets and a car park; the main entrance has a 2.1m height barrier. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/leavesden-country-park)
- **Manor House Grounds play area**, Abbots Langley. A short walk from the High Street, the play area has a zip wire, jungle swing, flying saucer and a climbing tower with a tube slide. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/news/epic-play-at-manor-house-grounds)
- **King George V Recreation Ground**, Shepherds Lane, Mill End, Rickmansworth. A children's play area, a skate area, a floodlit multi-use games area, an outdoor fitness zone and a circular walk. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/king-george-v-recreation-ground)
- **King George Recreation Ground**, Bushey. A Green Flag park with play areas for younger and older children, toilets and a tropical-island-themed splash park. The splash park is seasonal: Hertsmere's page lists it as open daily 10am to 6pm from 1 May to 13 September, with short closures during the day to reset the water. [Details](https://www.hertsmere.gov.uk/parks-and-leisure/parks-open-spaces/splash-parks)

## Splash parks (seasonal)

- **Cassiobury Park paddling pools**, Watford. Two splash pools plus splash pads, water jets and fountains, with changing facilities, toilets and a café kiosk. Entry is free. Half the places in each session can be booked through the Everyone Active app and the rest are for walk-ins, with one booked session per family per day. The pools are closed for the season now and the council says they'll reopen in spring 2027. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/paddling-pools)

## Nature reserves and walks

- **Rickmansworth Aquadrome**, Frogmoor Lane, Rickmansworth. Two lakes (Batchworth and Bury), each with a circular walk that is mostly solid, level and wide enough for wheelchairs. There are also two play areas, a dog-free picnic area with more play equipment, a café and toilets. The car park is free, and the council says it has no plans to charge. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/rickmansworth-aquadrome) · [Our listing](/activities/rickmansworth-aquadrome/)
- **Whippendell Woods and the River Gade**, Cassiobury Park, Watford. Around half of Cassiobury is woodland, including the ancient Whippendell Woods. The River Gade, a rare chalk river, runs alongside the canal through a Local Nature Reserve where you might spot dragonflies, herons and kingfishers. Some paths get wet, so take wellies. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/cassiobury-park-3/cassiobury-park-activities/6)
- **Croxley Common Moor**, Croxley Green. A 100-acre Local Nature Reserve and SSSI with more than 250 types of plant, lots of yellow meadow ant hills and green woodpeckers, and cattle grazing from April. You can walk in from Mill Lane or the Moor Lane Crossing off Tolpits Lane, about 100 yards from Croxley station. There's no car park on site. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/croxley-common-moor)
- **Chorleywood House Estate**, Chorleywood. Three short circular walks (1km, 1.2km and 1.5km) go through gardens, wildflower meadows, woodland, the Dell Nature Reserve and along the River Chess. Parking is free opposite the tennis courts. The paths aren't surfaced and can get slippery in winter. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/chorleywood-house-estate)
- **Oxhey Woods**, South Oxhey. This 100-hectare Local Nature Reserve is the largest in Three Rivers. A 1km circular route from the car park (at Prestwick Road and Oxhey Drive) has a firm surface suitable for buggies and passes wildlife sculptures. There are also longer trails and an orienteering course. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/oxhey-woods)

## Canal walks

- **Grand Union Canal at Cassiobury Park**, Watford. Walk the towpath, watch boats go through Iron Bridge Lock and have a picnic by the hump-backed bridge, with the playground nearby. The Canal & River Trust's visitor guide suggests allowing 2–3 hours, and says entry is free though some facilities and boat trips may be charged. [Details](https://canalrivertrust.org.uk/canals-and-rivers/places-to-visit/watford-cassiobury-park)

## Museums

- **Bushey Museum and Art Gallery**, Rudolph Road, Bushey. A free museum of local history with changing exhibitions and an art collection. Open Thursday to Sunday, 11am to 4pm. [Details](https://www.hertsmere.gov.uk/parks-and-leisure/museums-and-theatres/bushey-museum-and-art-gallery)
- **Three Rivers Museum**, Basing House, High Street, Rickmansworth. A small local history museum with free admission (donations welcome). Open Wednesday to Friday 2pm to 4pm, and Saturday 10am to 2pm. [Details](https://trmt.org.uk/)

## Libraries

- **Hertfordshire Libraries: Baby Rhyme Time and Storytime**, Watford, Rickmansworth and other branches. Baby Rhyme Time is a half-hour of songs and rhymes for babies and toddlers, and Storytime is an interactive story session for pre-schoolers. Both are free, and donations are welcome. Storytime doesn't need booking, but some libraries use Eventbrite for Rhyme Time, so check your branch's listing before you go. Children don't pay for library membership. [Details](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/events-and-things-to-do/library-events.aspx) · [Watford Library](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/library-opening-hours/watford-library.aspx) · [Rickmansworth Library](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/library-opening-hours/rickmansworth-library.aspx)

## Regular free events

- **Leavesden junior parkrun**, Leavesden Country Park, Abbots Langley. A free, timed 2k run for 4 to 14-year-olds every Sunday at 9am. [Details](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/leavesden-juniors/)
- **Cassiobury parkrun and Rickmansworth parkrun**, Watford and Rickmansworth. Free 5k runs every Saturday at 9am, in Cassiobury Park and at the Rickmansworth Aquadrome. Register once before your first run. [Cassiobury](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/cassiobury/) · [Rickmansworth](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/rickmansworth/)

## Good to know

- The Museum of Watford's current building is closed while it moves into Watford Town Hall, which is due to happen in 2027. Check [its website](https://watfordmuseum.org.uk/) for updates.
- Some favourites aren't free but don't cost much: the Watford Miniature Railway in Cassiobury Park is a [small cost per ride](/activities/watford-miniature-railway/), and [Langleybury Children's Farm](/activities/langleybury-childrens-farm/) in Kings Langley sells timed tickets.
- Nature reserves and woodland paths can get muddy after rain, so wellies are a good idea from autumn to spring.
