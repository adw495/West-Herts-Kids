## Watford

- **Cassiobury Park**, Gade Avenue, Watford. Watford's 250-acre park has two children's playgrounds, a free water play park with two splash pools, splash pads, water jets and fountains, the miniature railway, two outdoor gyms, a bouncy castle and ice cream van in summer, and woodland and canal walks. The paddling pools have closed for the season and the council says they'll reopen in spring 2027. There are two cafés (Cafe in the Park at the Tea Pavilion, daily 9am to 4pm, and the Cedar Café at the Cassiobury Park Hub, daily 8am to 5pm), and toilets with baby changing at both, plus a Changing Places toilet at the Hub. Cassiobury Park car park is free for up to 2 hours, then charged, with a 2.1m height barrier. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/345/cassiobury-park-and-whippendell-wood) · [Cafés](https://www.watford.gov.uk/cassiobury-park-3/cassiobury-park-activities/2) · [Toilets](https://www.watford.gov.uk/cassiobury-park-3/cassiobury-park/2) · [Paddling pools](https://www.watford.gov.uk/paddling-pools) · [Parking](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/25/cassiobury-park-car-park) · [Our listing](/activities/cassiobury-park/) · [Miniature railway](/activities/watford-miniature-railway/)
- **Oxhey Park and Oxhey Activity Park**, Wiggenhall Road and Eastbury Road, Watford. The activity park side has a playground with play trails, a jungle swing and slides, and the council says most of the equipment is ideal for children with disabilities, including sensory play. There's also a skate park with a pump track, free outdoor table tennis around the play areas (bring your own bats and ball), the riverside Tasty Bean café and a Changing Places toilet in the café. It's free and open daily; car parking is limited and Bushey station is 0.4 miles away. The Eastbury Road side has another children's playground, an outdoor gym and a mini football pitch, with on-street parking. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/oxhey-activity-park/oxhey-activity-park-1) · [Play facilities](https://www.watford.gov.uk/oxhey-activity-park/oxhey-activity-park-1/3) · [Oxhey Park](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/373/oxhey-park)
- **Woodside Playing Fields and Alban Wood**, Horseshoe Lane, Garston. A Green Flag park with a children's playground, an outdoor gym, toilets open dawn till dusk, an ice cream van in summer and woodland in a Local Nature Reserve. There's a car park, and Watford Leisure Centre Woodside is on site. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/387/woodside-playing-fields-and-alban-wood) · [Leisure centre](/activities/watford-leisure-centre-woodside/)
- **Garston Park**, Coates Way, Garston. A children's playground with accessible equipment, an outdoor gym, a small skate park, two table tennis tables, woodland and a stream. Parking is on the street. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/355/garston-park)
- **Callowland Recreation Ground**, Ashby Road, North Watford. A Green Flag park with a children's playground, a multi-use games area with basketball nets and football goals, an outdoor gym and two table tennis tables. Parking is on the street. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/344/callowland-recreation-ground)
- **Harebreaks Recreation Ground and Harebreaks Wood**, The Harebreaks, North Watford. The council lists both a children's playground and an adventure playground here, plus an outdoor gym and woodland that's a Local Nature Reserve. Parking is on the street. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/358/harebreaks-recreation-ground-and-harebreaks-wood)
- **Harwoods Recreation Ground**, Vicarage Road, West Watford. A children's playground, an adventure playground, a multi-use games area, an outdoor gym and two table tennis tables. Parking is on the street in a controlled parking zone. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/359/harwoods-recreation-ground)
- **King George V Playing Fields**, Tolpits Lane, Watford. A children's playground, a multi-use sports court, a six-station fitness trail and a table tennis table, with a car park. The fields connect to the Ebury Way. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/362/king-george-v-playing-fields)
- **Meriden Park**, Meriden Way, Garston. A children's playground, an outdoor gym, a multi-use games area and two table tennis tables, with a car park. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/366/meriden-park)
- **Knutsford Playing Fields**, Radlett Road, Watford. A children's playground, an outdoor gym and a viewing deck over the River Colne, with a car park. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/363/knutsford-playing-fields)
- **Other Watford playgrounds**: the council also lists children's playgrounds at Leavesden Green Recreation Ground (with a car park), Lea Farm Recreation Ground, Goodwood Recreation Ground, Orchard Park, Riverside Recreation Ground, Waterfields Recreation Ground, Watford Fields and East Drive, plus smaller neighbourhood play areas. [Full list](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory/7/parks-open-spaces-and-playgrounds/category/12)

## Rickmansworth, Mill End and Maple Cross

- **Rickmansworth Aquadrome and Ebury play area**, Frogmoor Lane and Riverside Drive, Rickmansworth. The Aquadrome has two play areas, including a natural play space with tree-trunk climbing, net climbers and crawl spaces, and a dog-free picnic area with more play equipment. The Ebury play area on the edge of the Aquadrome was refurbished and reopened in July 2026, with a zip wire, a junior multi-play with tube slide, a hilltop tower, a climbing wall, inclusive, toddler and junior swings, crawling tunnels and a sand pit; a pedestrian bridge takes you into the nature reserve. There's a café, toilets and a free car park (8am to 6pm from October to March; toilets close 30 minutes earlier). The council says asbestos remediation works start on 12 October, so check its page before you go. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/rickmansworth-aquadrome) · [Play areas](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/rickmansworth-moor-park-play-areas) · [Our listing](/activities/rickmansworth-aquadrome/)
- **Scotsbridge play area**, Park Road, Rickmansworth. A landscaped playground with a zip wire, trampoline, space net, gyro spiral, climbing post, dish roundabout, swings, a basket swing and sand play. The council lists a car park nearby. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/rickmansworth-moor-park-play-areas)
- **King George V Recreation Ground**, Shepherds Lane, Mill End. Junior and senior multi-play units, a zip wire, a trampoline, a sand play area, an adventure trail, swings and a roundabout, with parkour facilities nearby. The recreation ground also has a skate area, an outdoor fitness zone and a floodlit multi-use games area, and the council lists a car park nearby. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/mill-end-maple-cross-play-area) · [Park](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/king-george-v-recreation-ground)
- **Eastbury play area**, Batchworth Lane, Eastbury. A natural play space designed with help from local school pupils, with a tower with den and slide, a rope and log tangle with monkey rings, a willow tunnel, a spinner bowl and swings including a basket swing. The recreation ground also has tennis courts, an outdoor fitness zone and car parking. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/rickmansworth-moor-park-play-areas) · [Park](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/eastbury-recreation-ground)
- **Denham Way Playing Fields and Hornhill**, Maple Cross. Denham Way has toddler and junior multi-play, a crawling tunnel mound, swings and a roundabout, alongside a street skate area, bump track and outdoor fitness zone. There's a car park (WD3 9SL) but no public toilets. Hornhill play area on Hornhill Road has a zip wire, a trampoline, a climbing unit and balance logs. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/mill-end-maple-cross-play-area) · [Denham Way](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/denham-way-playing-fields)

## Croxley Green

- **Baldwins Lane play area**, Baldwins Lane, Croxley Green. The council calls this one of its flagship playgrounds, with equipment for all ages: a zip wire, a suspension bridge, a trampoline, an embankment slide, a crawling tunnel, a basket roundabout, and flat, cradle and basket swings. The green space around it has a skate area, an outdoor fitness zone, tennis courts and table tennis tables. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/croxley-green-play-area) · [Park](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/baldwins-lane)
- **Barton Way play area**, Barton Way, Croxley Green. This play area has been refurbished, and the council's page says it was due to reopen on 26 March 2026. A car park, the library, a multi-use games area and an outdoor gym are nearby. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/croxley-green-play-area) · [Park](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/barton-way)
- **Cassiobridge play area**, Watford Road, Croxley Green. Swings, slides and play units for toddlers and young children, including a multi-play tower, toddler swings and a musical play panel, plus an activity trail and a 5-a-side goal area for older children. It has a self-closing gate. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/croxley-green-play-area)

## Chorleywood

- **The Swillet**, Heronsgate Road, Chorleywood. A play area with toddler and junior multi-play units, a sand pit, a roundabout, a see-saw, a climbing frame, stepping stones, a basket swing and hammock, and flat and cradle swings, with a self-closing gate, benches and picnic tables. The green space also has a cycle track and a circular walk. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/chorleywood-sarratt-play-area) · [Park](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/the-swillet)

## South Oxhey and Carpenders Park

- **South Oxhey Playing Fields**, South Oxhey. Two play areas, off the Ashburnham Drive and Hayling Road entrances. Ashburnham Drive has zip wires between the trees, a climbing wall, a suspension bridge, a trampoline, an embankment slide, hammocks, net climbers and an activity trail. Hayling Road has a zip wire, a climbing unit, sand play, a maze and a willow tunnel; the council is asking local people for views on refurbishing it. The playing fields also have a skate park, a multi-use games area and table tennis. The car park is off Green Lane (WD19 4LT) and there are no public toilets. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/south-oxhey-playing-fields) · [Play areas](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/south-oxhey-carpenders-park-play-areas)
- **Oulton Way play area**, Romily Drive Open Space, Carpenders Park. A zip wire, a net climber, a trampoline, a sand pit with a sand excavator, a crawling tunnel and a roundabout. The open space also has a skate park and a basketball hoop. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/south-oxhey-carpenders-park-play-areas) · [Park](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/romily-drive-open-space)
- **Greenfields Avenue play area**, Greenfield Avenue, South Oxhey. Run by Watford Rural Parish Council, with climbing trees, balance logs, a zip wire, a jungle swing, a trampoline, a slide and a mound with a bridge. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/south-oxhey-carpenders-park-play-areas)

## Abbots Langley and Leavesden

- **Leavesden Country Park**, College Road, Abbots Langley. The play area has a tower and slide, crawling tunnels, a willow tunnel, a "flying carpet" swing, toddler and basket swings, an aeroplane climber and theatre stage play. The park also has the Woodlands Café, toilets near the café (weekdays 9.30am to 6pm, weekends 9.30am to 4pm), woodland walks and a heritage sculpture trail. The car park is open 6.30am to 10pm and has a 2.1m height barrier at the main entrance. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/leavesden-country-park) · [Play area](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/abbots-langley-play-area)
- **Manor House Grounds play area**, Gallows Hill Lane, Abbots Langley. Run by Abbots Langley Parish Council, with a zip wire, a pendulum jungle swing, a play tower with tube slide and bridge, climbing and crawling nets, a flying saucer roundabout, an embankment slide and inclusive, toddler and junior swings. There's a multi-games area for skating and tennis nearby. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/abbots-langley-play-area)
- **South Way play area**, South Way, Abbots Langley. A zip wire, a trampoline, a jungle climber, a sand pit, a balance trail, climbing posts, a slide and swings, with an outdoor gym nearby. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/abbots-langley-play-area)
- **Bedmond play area**, Toms Lane, Bedmond. A zip wire, a large play tower, a climbing net unit, a flying saucer roundabout, a basket swing and a seesaw, next to football pitches and a small basketball court. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/abbots-langley-play-area)

## Kings Langley

- **Primrose Hill play area**, Primrose Hill, Kings Langley. Run by Abbots Langley Parish Council, with a pirate-themed toddler unit, a submarine-themed junior unit, a trampoline, a Viking swing, a basket swing, toddler swings, a climbing net and an accessible picnic bench. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/abbots-langley-play-area)
- **Gade View Gardens play area**, Gade View Gardens, near Kings Langley station. A small play area with a basket swing and a multi-play unit with slide for toddlers and young children. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/abbots-langley-play-area)

## Bushey

- **King George Recreation Ground**, King George Avenue, Bushey. A Green Flag park with a play area for 2 to 12-year-olds (pyramid net, double cableway, multi-units with slides, swings, a trim trail, a tyre swing and a wheelchair-accessible roundabout), climbing equipment for older children and a tropical-island-themed splash park with three bays for different ages. The splash park opened daily from 10am to 6pm between 1 May and 13 September this year, with short closures to reset the water. There's a café, toilets (8am to 3.30pm in winter), a free car park off King George Avenue and overflow parking off Chiltern Avenue. [Details](https://www.parksherts.co.uk/parks/king-george-recreation-ground/) · [Splash park](https://www.hertsmere.gov.uk/parks-and-leisure/parks-open-spaces/splash-parks)
- **Fishers Park**, North Bushey. Next to the Fishers Field Local Nature Reserve, with a play area that has a four-way springer, a slide, a speed gyro roundabout, a balance beam, a basket swing and a trampoline. Parking is limited, on Fishers Close off Bendysh Road. [Details](https://www.parksherts.co.uk/parks/fishers-field-local-nature-reserve-fishers-park/)
