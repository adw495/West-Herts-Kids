## Trampolining, ninja and climbing

- **Flip Out Watford**, lower level, atria Watford, Watford. Every party includes Flip Out socks, drinks and 30 minutes in a party room with pizza and crisps, with a minimum of 10 children. The Weekday Classic (term-time weekdays) is £21.99 a child for an hour in the arena, the party room, then another 30 minutes of play. At weekends and in the holidays, the Classic is £24.99 and the Premium (with slush, party bag and a free return visit) £29.99. The Ultimate is £39.99. There's also a Laser Quest party (10 to 15 children, minimum height 1.2m) from £23.99 off-peak, and a Little Flippers party for children aged 5 and under at £24.99, which covers one adult and one child. [Details](https://www.flipout.co.uk/locations/watford/birthday-parties) · [Our listing](/activities/flip-out-watford/)
- **Ninja Warrior UK Watford**, Woodside Leisure Park, Watford. Parties are for children over 5, with a minimum of 10, and last an hour. All packages include pizza, ice lollies, unlimited squash, Ninja Warrior UK socks and a free return voucher. The Ninja Party (£22.95 a child, Monday to Thursday in term time) uses a shared party space; the Hero Ninja Party (£28.95, any day) adds your own party room and a dedicated host; the Ultimate Ninja Party (£32.95) adds Ninja TAG and slush. [Details](https://ninjawarrioruk.co.uk/watford/parties/) · [Our listing](/activities/ninja-warrior-uk-watford/)
- **Rock Up Watford**, atria Watford, Watford. Climbing parties are for ages 4 and up and last 2 hours (a 30-minute briefing, an hour of climbing and 30 minutes to celebrate), with a minimum of 8. The Summit Party is £24.95 a person (£22.95 on term-time weekdays) with pizza, refreshments, a party host and a Leap of Faith for the birthday child. The Ultimate Climbing Party (£30.95, or £28.95 on term-time weekdays) adds party bags and a team challenge. A Saver Party (minimum 6) is £21.95 with slush instead of food. For younger children there's a Toddler Party for 4 and under (£20.95, mornings) and a Soft Play Party for 8 and under (£19.95), both with pizza. [Details](https://www.rock-up.co.uk/locations/watford/climbing-parties-in-watford) · [Our listing](/activities/rock-up-watford/)

## Soft play

- **Parents Paradise**, Greatham Road Industrial Estate, Bushey. All parties last 2 hours, need at least 12 children, and include exclusive use of a private room, a party host and freshly prepared party food in the last 30 minutes. The Paradise Play Party (ages 1–12) is £18 a child Tuesday to Thursday or £24.50 Friday to Sunday and in the holidays, with about an hour and a half of soft play. Unicorn and Forever My Bear parties (ages 3+) and a Slime Party (ages 5+, up to 18 children) add a 30-minute making session and cost £25.50 a child Tuesday to Thursday or £32 at other times. A 50% non-refundable deposit is needed. [Details](https://parentsparadise.co.uk/parties/) · [Our listing](/activities/parents-paradise-bushey/)

## Bowling and karting

- **Hollywood Bowl Watford Woodside**, Woodside Leisure Park, Watford. Kids' birthday parties are for ages 5 to 10. The Kids Birthday Party (from £11 a child, groups of 6 or more) includes an hour of bowling, a kids' meal served to the lanes, unlimited squash and a free game voucher each. The Kid+ party (from £15.50) serves the meal in a seating area decorated with balloons, and the 90-minute Premium party (from £25, groups of 10 to 12) adds prize tickets and a free bowling voucher for every child. [Details](https://www.hollywoodbowl.co.uk/watford-woodside/groups-parties)
- **Hollywood Bowl Watford Harlequin**, atria Watford, Watford. The same Kids (from £11 a child) and Kid+ (from £15.50) bowling parties for ages 5 to 10 and groups of 6 or more, and the centre also lists a Kids Mini Golf Birthday Party with an hour of mini golf, a kids' meal, unlimited squash and a game voucher each. [Details](https://www.hollywoodbowl.co.uk/watford-harlequin/groups-parties) · [Mini golf parties](https://www.hollywoodbowl.co.uk/watford-atria/groups-parties)
- **TeamSport Watford**, Greatham Road Industrial Estate, Bushey. The Awesome Kids Karting Party is for 8 to 16-year-olds and gives your group the track to itself for two races, with smaller Cadet karts for younger drivers, medals for everyone, a gold race suit for the birthday child and a reserved table in the bar. It's best for groups of more than 5 drivers. Prices are per party and cheapest Monday to Thursday before 7pm; a pizza-making party can be added (not usually on Saturdays). Arrive 40 minutes early, and drivers need a balaclava (£3.99 at the venue if you don't have one). [Details](https://www.team-sport.co.uk/parties-and-events/kids-birthday-parties) · [Our listing](/activities/teamsport-watford/)

## Arts and creative

- **art-K**, 160–162 Watford Road, Croxley Green. Art parties for children turning 6 and over cost £30 to £40 a child depending on location, with a minimum of 10. Studio parties last 2 hours 45 minutes, with about 2 hours of art, exclusive use of the studio, an art instructor and materials, and every child takes their artwork home. You bring the food and cake. In term time, parties run on weekdays before 3pm, Saturdays after 4pm and Sundays after 12pm; in the school holidays, weekdays after 4pm and all day at weekends. art-K can also come to you. [Details](https://art-k.co.uk/birthday-parties) · [Our listing](/activities/art-k-croxley-green/)
- **Hertfordshire Libraries**, Croxley Green Library and Watford Library. You can hire a private space in the library for a children's party during opening hours and bring your own decorations, food, drink and entertainment. Optional self-led activity packages start from £72, including LEGO, DUPLO (with a Storytime or Baby Rhyme Time led by staff), a Gravitrax marble run with Sphero Mini, and an Intelino smart train. [Details](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/other-library-services/rooms-and-spaces-to-hire-in-libraries/childrens-parties-in-hertfordshire-libraries.aspx)

## Sports parties

- **We Make Footballers Watford**, at a venue you choose. Football parties for ages 4 to 12 last an hour, led by DBS-checked coaches who bring the equipment and set up and pack away. Silver covers 1 to 12 children with 1 coach, Gold 12 to 24 with 2, and Platinum 24 or more with 3. Contact them for prices; venue hire isn't included, but they can suggest a venue. [Details](https://wemakefootballers.com/uk/watford/events/birthday-parties) · [Our listing](/activities/we-make-footballers-watford/)
- **Xtra Time**, Watford. Sports-themed parties led by its coaches, for children from Reception upwards, with themes including multisports and magic. Prices start at £220 for up to 16 children for a 2-hour party (up to 30 at extra cost). [Details](https://www.xtratime.co.uk/birthday-parties-2/) · [Our listing](/activities/xtra-time-watford/)
