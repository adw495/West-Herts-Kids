Rickmansworth and Croxley Green sit where the Grand Union Canal meets two big lakes and a nature reserve, so there's a lot to do outdoors, much of it free. We've checked every place below against the council's, venue's or organiser's own website, and added a short list of regular classes and clubs at the end.

## Lakes, sailing and the Aquadrome

- **Rickmansworth Aquadrome**, Frogmoor Lane, Rickmansworth. A Local Nature Reserve with two lakes, Batchworth and Bury, and paths around them that the council describes as fully accessible. There are two play areas, including the refurbished Ebury Play Area, a dog-free picnic area with more play equipment, a café and toilets. Parking is free (8am to 6pm from October to March, 8am to 9:30pm from April to September). Swimming, personal paddleboards and barbecues aren't allowed. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/rickmansworth-aquadrome) · [Our listing](/activities/rickmansworth-aquadrome/)
- **Bury Lake Young Mariners**, Rickmansworth Aquadrome. A volunteer-run youth sailing charity on Bury Lake. Children aged 7 to 9 can try Junior Days, and RYA youth sailing courses start from age 9. Autumn half-term courses are open for booking on the club's website. [Details](https://blym.org.uk/) · [Our listing](/activities/bury-lake-young-mariners/)
- **Rickmansworth Sailing Club**, Troy Lake, West Hyde. The club's Trojans sessions for 4 to 8-year-olds run on set Saturdays from 11am to 4:30pm, mainly for members, though the club says interested youngsters may be welcomed. It's also part of the RYA OnBoard programme for 8 to 18-year-olds. [Details](https://rscsailing.org/ng/youth-schemes/) · [Our listing](/activities/rickmansworth-sailing-club/)

## Canal and boat trips

- **Rickmansworth Waterways Trust: boat trips from Batchworth Lock**, Rickmansworth. The trust's boat, Pride of Batchworth, runs short trips from Batchworth Lock to Stockers Lock and back, taking around 30 to 40 minutes. Prices are £6 for adults, £3 for under-16s and £15 for a family of two adults and two children. Under-16s must be with an adult, and the trust asks you to phone ahead (01923 778382) as a trip can't be guaranteed if you just turn up. [Details](https://rwt.org.uk/canal-cruises/trips/)
- **Halloween Story Boats**, Batchworth Lock, Rickmansworth. On 29, 30 and 31 October 2026, boats run hourly from 10am (last boat 3pm). Children hear stories on the canal ("not too scary"), then have games and crafts in the Education Centre. Tickets are £12 for children and £9 for adults, including refreshments on board. [Details](https://rwt.org.uk/news/halloween-story-boats-booking-now-open/)
- **Café@Lock 81**, between Batchworth and Chess Locks. A canalside café open Wednesday to Sunday, 10:30am to 5:30pm, and on bank holiday Mondays. [Details](https://rwt.org.uk/around-batchworth/cafe/)

## Parks and nature reserves

- **Croxley Common Moor**, Croxley Green. A 100-acre Local Nature Reserve and SSSI with more than 250 types of plant, yellow meadow ant hills and green woodpeckers, and cows grazing from around April. Walk in from Mill Lane or the Moor Lane Crossing off Tolpits Lane, about 100 yards from Croxley station. There's no car park on site. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/croxley-common-moor)
- **King George V Recreation Ground**, Shepherds Lane, Mill End, Rickmansworth. A play area, a skate area, football pitches, a floodlit multi-use games area, an outdoor fitness zone and a circular walk. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/king-george-v-recreation-ground)
- **Chorleywood House Estate**, Chorleywood. Three short circular walks (1km, 1.2km and 1.5km) through gardens, wildflower meadows, woodland and the Dell Nature Reserve down to the River Chess, plus free turn-up-and-play tennis courts. Parking is free opposite the tennis courts. The paths aren't surfaced and can get slippery in winter. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/chorleywood-house-estate)
- **Cassiobury Park**, Gade Avenue, Watford. Watford's Green Flag park has two children's playgrounds, a café, the miniature railway, woodland, the River Gade and the Grand Union Canal. It's a Local Nature Reserve with a bird hide and a nature trail. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/directory-record/345/cassiobury-park-and-whippendell-wood) · [Our listing](/activities/cassiobury-park/)

## Museum, theatre and cinema

- **Three Rivers Museum**, Basing House, High Street, Rickmansworth. A small local history museum. Admission is free and donations are welcome. Open Wednesday to Friday 2pm to 4pm, and Saturday 10am to 2pm. [Details](https://trmt.org.uk/)
- **Watersmeet**, High Street, Rickmansworth. Rickmansworth's theatre puts on live shows and films. October half-term family events include Disney Junior Cinema Club (25 October), the film Hook (27 October), Magic & Mayhem (28 October), The Utterly Revolting Science Show (29 October) and the film The Beast from Mossy Bottom (1 November). This year's pantomime is Peter Pan. Check the website for times and prices. [Half term](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/october-half-term) · [Pantomime](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/peter-pan)

## Swimming and sport

- **William Penn Leisure Centre**, Mill End, Rickmansworth. Run by Everyone Active, with a 25-metre main pool and a smaller teaching pool, children's swimming lessons, public swims and junior gym sessions. Junior membership is £22.99 a month. [Details](https://www.everyoneactive.com/centre/william-penn-leisure-centre/) · [Our listing](/activities/william-penn-leisure-centre/)

## Libraries

- **Rickmansworth and Croxley Green libraries**. Hertfordshire Libraries runs free Baby Rhyme Time (30 minutes of songs and rhymes for babies and toddlers) and Storytime for pre-schoolers, as well as LEGO workshops for 5 to 11-year-olds. Storytime doesn't need booking, but some libraries use Eventbrite for Rhyme Time, so check your branch's events listing first. Rickmansworth Library is on the High Street and is closed on Wednesdays and Sundays. Croxley Green Library is on Barton Way and has both staffed and self-service hours (self-service only on Thursdays), so check the times before you go. [Events](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/events-and-things-to-do/library-events.aspx) · [Rickmansworth Library](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/library-opening-hours/rickmansworth-library.aspx) · [Croxley Green Library](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/library-opening-hours/croxley-green-library.aspx)

## Getting there

- **By Tube and train:** Rickmansworth station is served by the Metropolitan line and Chiltern Railways, and Croxley station by the Metropolitan line. Both are in TfL fare zone 7. [Rickmansworth station](https://tfl.gov.uk/tube/stop/940GZZLURKW/rickmansworth-underground-station) · [Croxley station](https://tfl.gov.uk/tube/stop/940GZZLUCXY/croxley-underground-station)
- **Parking:** the Aquadrome and the Chorleywood House Estate have free car parks (see above). Croxley Common Moor has no car park.
