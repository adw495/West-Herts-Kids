## The providers

- **Water Babies**, Meadow Wood School pool, Coldharbour Lane, Bushey. Weekly 30-minute lessons with a parent in the water, grouped by age: 0 to 1, 1 to 2, 2 to 3 and 3 to 4, plus Swimvincibles for children aged 4 and over. Water Babies says the pool is kept at a minimum of 29°C (32°C for babies under 12 weeks). Prices and class times aren't on the pool page; you search by postcode and your child's date of birth. [Details](https://www.waterbabies.co.uk/baby-swimming/hertfordshire/meadow-wood-school-pool/) · [Our listing](/activities/water-babies-bushey-meadow-wood/)
- **Watford Swim School**, Stud Green, Garston, Watford. Takes babies from 4 months. Parent & Baby lessons are 30 minutes, cost £17 per parent and baby each week, run in school term time only and are daytime lessons (not at weekends), with up to four children per teacher. Parents don't have to be in the water once a child is 3. The school's own pool is in a garden in Stud Green (it also teaches at Abbots Hill School in Hemel Hempstead), and it runs a waiting list for most slots. [Details](https://watfordswimschool.com/lessons-prices/) · [FAQ](https://watfordswimschool.com/faq/) · [Our listing](/activities/watford-swim-school/)
- **Swim4Life Watford**, Watford Grammar School for Girls, Watford. A family-run swim school teaching small classes for children aged one and over, in 30 and 45-minute lessons that follow the Swim England stages. Its 2026 schedule has a Parent + Toddler class (alongside Stages 1 to 3) on Saturdays from 11:30am to 12:30pm. The pool is reached through the blue gate on Vicarage Road. Prices aren't published; you register through its booking page. [Details](https://swim4lifewatford.co.uk/)
- **Everyone Active**, Watford Leisure Centre Central, Watford Leisure Centre Woodside, William Penn Leisure Centre (Rickmansworth) and South Oxhey Leisure Centre. Everyone Active's lessons start from 4 months. Its adult and child classes for 4 to 36 months use songs, games and support to build water confidence with you in the water, and from age 3 (if not yet in full-time school) children can move to pre-school lessons without a parent. Its website doesn't say which of the four local centres run the adult and child classes, so check with your centre or the Everyone Active app. [Details](https://everyoneactive.com/content-hub/swimming/childrens-swimming-lessons) · [Central](/activities/watford-leisure-centre-central/) · [Woodside](/activities/watford-leisure-centre-woodside/) · [William Penn](/activities/william-penn-leisure-centre/) · [South Oxhey](/activities/south-oxhey-leisure-centre/)
- **SwimFitz**, Rickmansworth, Chorleywood, Croxley Green and Watford. If you have access to a private pool, SwimFitz teaches babies from 6 months one-to-one, with a parent in the water throughout. It works seven days a week (from 7am on weekdays and 9am at weekends) across the WD3, WD18, HA6, WD4 and WD5 postcodes. Prices are by arrangement and confirmed at booking. [Details](https://swimfitz.com/home-swimming-lessons-rickmansworth) · [Our listing](/activities/swimfitz-home-swimming-lessons/)
