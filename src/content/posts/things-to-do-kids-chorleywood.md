Chorleywood is at its best outdoors: a council-run estate with short family walks down to the river Chess, a large common that's a nature reserve, and a well-equipped play area. We've checked everything below on the council's or provider's own website.

## Parks, play and open spaces

- **Chorleywood House Estate**, off the A404 (WD3 5SL). A Local Nature Reserve with formal gardens, the Dell nature reserve, picnic spots and meadows down to the river Chess. There are three short, self-guided circular walks: the **Park walk (1km)**, the **Wood walk (1.2km)**, known for its bluebells in spring, and the **Valley walk (1.5km)** down to the river. The **public tennis courts are free** (turn up and play), and **parking is free** opposite the tennis courts, with more by the football pitches. The paths aren't surfaced and some areas get slippery, and cattle graze the meadows, so keep little ones close. [Details](https://threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/chorleywood-house-estate)
- **Chorleywood Common**. A Local Nature Reserve looked after by the parish council, with **seven restored ponds**, heathland and a chalk meadow with wildflowers and butterflies. There's a café on the Common, the **Cafe on the Common** (WD3 5SG), and the parish council lists parking nearby at Christchurch, the Cricket Club, the A404 parking area and Chorleywood House Estate ([parking details](https://www.chorleywood-pc.gov.uk/council_events/scary-skeleton-trail-29-october-10am/)). From 1 March to 31 July, the parish council asks for dogs to be kept on a short 2m lead in some areas. [Details](https://www.chorleywood-pc.gov.uk/services/chorleywood-common/)
- **Swillet play area**, Heronsgate Road. The Three Rivers play area for Chorleywood, with **toddler and junior multi-play units**, a sand pit, a roundabout, a see-saw, a climbing frame, stepping stones, flat-seat swings, inclusive cradle swings, and a basket swing and hammock. There are benches and picnic tables, and a self-closing gate. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/chorleywood-sarratt-play-area)

## Library

- **Chorleywood Community Library**, Lower Road (WD3 5LB). Run with volunteers. It's open Monday and Thursday 9.30am to 6pm, Wednesday and Friday 2pm to 6pm and Saturday 9.30am to 2pm, and closed Tuesdays, Sundays and bank holidays. It has wheelchair access and parking nearby, including disabled bays. [Opening hours](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/library-opening-hours/chorleywood-community-library.aspx)
- The library is also part of the Three Rivers **community toilet scheme**, with a disabled toilet and **baby change** during opening hours. [Details](https://threerivers.gov.uk/services/community-and-living/community-toilets)
- Hertfordshire libraries run free **Baby Rhyme Time** (half an hour, donations welcome; some libraries ask you to book) and free **Storytime** for pre-schoolers. Check the library events page for Chorleywood's current sessions. [Library events](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/events-and-things-to-do/library-events.aspx)

## Running

There's no parkrun in Chorleywood itself. Close by is **Rickmansworth parkrun**, a free timed 5k every Saturday at 9am at [Rickmansworth Aquadrome](/activities/rickmansworth-aquadrome/) ([details](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/rickmansworth/)). For 4 to 14-year-olds, **Leavesden junior parkrun** is a free 2k every Sunday at 9am at Leavesden Country Park ([details](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/leavesden-juniors/)).

## Events in 2026

- **Spooky Trail**, Chorleywood Common. Thursday 29 and Friday 30 October 2026, with timed slots on the hour from 10am to 2pm ([all slots](https://www.chorleywood-pc.gov.uk/council_events/)), starting near the Cafe on the Common. £3.50 per child plus a £1 admin fee; adults don't need a ticket, but children must be with an adult. It's buggy-friendly but can be muddy. [Details](https://www.chorleywood-pc.gov.uk/council_events/scary-skeleton-trail-29-october-10am/)

## Clubs and classes in Chorleywood

- **[Chorleywood Lawn Tennis Club juniors](/activities/chorleywood-lawn-tennis-club-juniors/)**: junior coaching for ages 3 to 18, from Tots Tennis to squads.
- **[Cygnets Art School](/activities/cygnets-art-school-chorleywood/)**: weekly art classes for 5 to 12-year-olds.
- **[1st Chorleywood Scouts](/activities/1st-chorleywood-scouts/)**: Beavers, Cubs and Scouts for ages 6 to 14.
- **[Hartbeeps](/activities/hartbeeps-rickmansworth-chorleywood-croxley/)**: multi-sensory music and play classes for babies, toddlers and pre-schoolers.
