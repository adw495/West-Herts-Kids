Finding activities where a child with special educational needs or a disability can simply join in, without anyone having to explain or apologise, takes time. This guide brings together what we could confirm locally, and every detail below comes from the organiser's own website. Places are often limited and details change, so **always contact the organiser before you go**.

## Sport and swimming

- **[Dolphina Gymnastics Club](/activities/dolphina-gymnastics-watford/)**, Garston, Watford: free SEND "stay and play" sessions with soft play and gymnastics equipment, just for children with SEND. The last Tuesday of every month, 12–1pm. Open to members and non-members, but spaces are very limited and booking is essential. [Details](https://dolphinagymnastics.com/index.php/colibri-7543/)
- **[Watford Gymnastics Club](/activities/watford-gymnastics-club/)**, Watford Business Park: runs SEND gymnastics classes, which had a waiting list when we checked. Contact the club to ask about places. [Details](https://www.watfordgymnastics.co.uk/watford-site)
- **Sea Lion Swimming Club**, Watford Leisure Centre Central: a volunteer-run club with exclusive use of the pool for disabled swimmers of any age and their families and carers. Saturdays 3:45–4:45pm, all year except Christmas. Membership is £45 a year (£90 for a family) with no weekly charge, and a free trial session is offered. A carer goes in the water with the swimmer, and there's a hoist. [Details](https://www.sealionswimmingclub.org.uk/)
- **Everett Rovers Inclusive Teams**, Dodd Road, Watford: pan-disability football for boys and girls aged 6 to 19, including players with Down's syndrome, autism, ADHD, learning difficulties and physical disabilities. Youth training is on Saturdays, 9–10am. Contact the club before your first session. [Details](https://everettroversfc.co.uk/our-teams/inclusive-teams/)
- **Colne Valley Special Sailors**, Rickmansworth Aquadrome: a volunteer charity offering sailing in adapted boats for disabled people of all ages, including autistic children and those with sensory difficulties, with a hoist available. The 2026 season ends on 21 October, so it's one to note for next spring. Annual membership applies and there's an application form. [Details](https://cvssailors.org.uk/)

## Theatre and days out

- **Watford Palace Theatre** runs **relaxed performances**: the lights stay on, loud noises and bright lights are softened, and you can move around and come and go. There's a chill-out space, a visual story sent in advance, and a free companion ticket for nearly all shows. This year's panto, *A Christmas Carol*, has relaxed performances on **Wednesday 9 December** and **Sunday 13 December** at 2:30pm. [Access information](https://watfordpalacetheatre.co.uk/about-us/your-visit/access/)
- **[Warner Bros. Studio Tour London](/activities/warner-bros-studio-tour-london/)**, Leavesden: the next **Relaxed Tour** is on **Saturday 5 December 2026**, with fewer visitors, adjusted lighting and quieter sound from 8am to 10am. On any day there are two sensory rooms, sensory support bags from the Information Desk, a changing room with a hoist, and one free companion ticket per paying disabled visitor (proof required). [Details](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/the-experience/whats-on/relaxed-tours/)

## Clubs and holiday schemes

- **Watford Mencap**, Langley Road, Watford: children and family services for children with learning disabilities, including Saturday clubs in term time, a youth club for 13–19s on Friday evenings, and holiday activity schemes in every school holiday. Some sessions can be paid for with Short Breaks hours. [Details](https://www.watfordmencap.org.uk/our-support-and-services)
- **Hertfordshire Short Breaks** (county council scheme): holiday and Saturday clubs for 5–19 year olds with SEND, run by approved local providers. You don't need a formal diagnosis; eligibility is based on DLA, PIP or a letter from a GP, SENCo or other professional. It's up to 40 hours a year at £3.50 an hour. You register on the county portal, then book with the provider. [Details](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/microsites/local-offer/courses-and-activities-in-hertfordshire/short-breaks.aspx)

## Good to know

- **Changing Places toilets** (with a hoist and an adult-sized changing bench) are at the **Cassiobury Park Hub** and **Woodside Playing Fields** in Watford, among others. Use the [official Changing Places map](https://www.changing-places.org) to find one near where you're going.
- The **Herts Card (SEND)** is a free discount card for 0–25 year olds, available when you join Hertfordshire's Additional Needs Database. [Details](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/microsites/local-offer/courses-and-activities-in-hertfordshire/hertfordshire-additional-needs-database.aspx)
- For more, see the [Hertfordshire SEND Local Offer events](https://events.hertfordshire.gov.uk/local-offer) and [Three Rivers Council's inclusive programme](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/inclusive-programme).
