Abbots Langley and Kings Langley sit side by side between Watford and Hemel Hempstead. Between them they have Leavesden Country Park, about a dozen play areas (three with zip wires), a Sunday junior parkrun and the Harry Potter studio tour on the doorstep. We've checked everything below on the council's, venue's or organiser's own website.

## Leavesden Country Park

**Leavesden Country Park**, College Road (WD5 0NX). The play area has a tower, slide, giant crawl tunnels, a "flying carpet" swing, an aeroplane climber and toddler swings. Nearby there's the **Woodlands Café**, toilets, an outdoor gym, tennis and a multi-use games area ([play area details](https://threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/abbots-langley-play-area)). The **Heritage Sculpture Trail** is suitable for buggies and wheelchairs and takes about an hour there and back. The car park is open 6.30am to 10pm daily, with a 2.1m height barrier. Cattle graze the Horses' Field in summer. [Details](https://threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/parks-open-spaces/leavesden-country-park)

**Leavesden junior parkrun** is a free, timed 2k for 4 to 14-year-olds here **every Sunday at 9am**. [Details](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/leavesden-juniors/)

## Play areas

All of these are on the [Three Rivers play areas page](https://threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play-areas/abbots-langley-play-area), which lists the equipment at each one.

- **Manor House Grounds**, Gallows Hill Lane, Abbots Langley. A **zip wire**, a play tower with a tube slide, inclusive swings, a flying-saucer roundabout, a jungle swing and climbing nets. Next to it is the parish council's floodlit, fenced **multi-use games area** for football and tennis ([details](https://www.abbotslangley-pc.gov.uk/services/multi-use-games-area/)).
- **Primrose Hill**, Kings Langley (WD4 8HY). A **pirate-themed** toddler unit, a submarine-themed junior unit, a trampoline, a Viking swing, a basket swing, a climbing net and an accessible picnic bench. The playing field isn't used for football because moles have made the ground uneven ([details](https://www.abbotslangley-pc.gov.uk/primrose-hill-playing-fields/)).
- **South Way**, Abbots Langley (WD5 0JL). A zip wire, a trampoline and a sand pit.
- **Bedmond**, Toms Lane. A zip wire and a play tower.
- **Ovaltine Drive**, Kings Langley (WD4 8SG). Spring animals, a toddler swing, a panel maze, a multi-play unit with a slide and a see-saw. Managed by Notting Hill Genesis.
- **Gade View Gardens** (WD4 8PH). An inclusive basket swing, a slide and a multi-play unit.
- There are also smaller play areas at **Abbotswood Park**, **Langley Lane**, **Dowding Way**, **Lemonfield Drive** and **Love Lane**.

**Parking:** the parish council's car parks at Manor House, Manor Lodge (High Street), Primrose Hill, South Way, Bedmond and Langleybury are **free for up to 3 hours** (no return within 3 hours, restrictions 9am to 4pm, no overnight parking). [Details](https://www.abbotslangley-pc.gov.uk/services/car-parks/)

**Toilets:** besides Leavesden Country Park, Henderson Hall on Abbots Langley High Street (WD5 0AR) has public toilets with baby change, Tuesday to Saturday 10am to 2pm. [Details](https://threerivers.gov.uk/services/community-and-living/community-toilets)

## Days out

- **[Warner Bros. Studio Tour London](/activities/warner-bros-studio-tour-london/)**, Leavesden (WD25 7LR). The Making of Harry Potter. 2026 prices are **£58.50 for adults (16+), £47 for children aged 5 to 15** and **£188 for a family**, and under-4s go free ([tickets](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/tickets/)). **[Dark Arts](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/the-experience/whats-on/dark-arts/)** runs until 8 November 2026, and **Hogwarts in the Snow** from 14 November 2026 to 17 January 2027 ([what's on](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/the-experience/whats-on/hogwarts-in-the-snow/)). Parking is free, and a free shuttle bus runs from Watford Junction ([getting there](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/plan-your-visit/getting-here/)).
- **[Langleybury Children's Farm](/activities/langleybury-childrens-farm/)**, near Kings Langley. Animals, a nature trail with a new zip wire, and play areas for under-5s and over-5s. Its last open day of 2026 was Saturday 24 October, so check [the farm's website](https://www.langleyburychildrensfarm.org.uk/) for 2027 opening. Entry has been £5.25 per adult and per child ([source](https://www.langleyburychildrensfarm.org.uk/copy-of-book-a-visit)), and every child over 1 needs an e-ticket ([FAQ](https://www.langleyburychildrensfarm.org.uk/faq-s)).

## Walks

- Abbots Langley Parish Council publishes **ten circular walks** of 1.7 to 7.9 miles, including a 5.6-mile route through Hunton Bridge, Langleybury and Kings Langley along the **Grand Union Canal**. [Walks](https://www.abbotslangley-pc.gov.uk/footpath-walks/)
- Kings Langley Parish Council has walk leaflets, including **A Walk Through History in Kings Langley**. [Maps and walks](https://kingslangley-pc.gov.uk/about/maps/)
