Hertfordshire's October half term runs from **Monday 26 to Friday 30 October 2026**, with Halloween falling on the Saturday straight after. Below are the trails, shows, pumpkin patches and one-off family events we've confirmed for 2026 on the organisers' own websites. Looking for childcare-style camps instead? See [our half-term holiday camps guide](/whats-on/october-half-term-2026-holiday-camps/).

## Trails and trick or treat

- **Trick or Treat Trail**, Watford High Street and The Parade, Watford. Monday 26 to Friday 30 October 2026, 10am to 4pm, free. Look for 10 large inflatable ghosts in shop windows, then pop in and say "Trick or treat" for a sweet treat. Fancy dress is encouraged. The trail is only on the High Street and The Parade, not on side roads or in the Harlequin. [Details](https://watfordtowncentre.com/event/trick-or-treat-trail-2026/)
- **Spooky Trail**, Chorleywood Common (near Cafe on the Common), Chorleywood. Thursday 29 and Friday 30 October 2026, with timed slots on the hour from 10am to 2pm. £3.50 per child plus a £1 admin fee; adults don't need a ticket, but every child must be with an adult. Follow a woodland trail decorated with skeletons and solve the hidden clues; it takes about 30 to 45 minutes. The ground is mostly flat and grassy and fine for buggies, but it can be muddy. Parking is at Christchurch, the Cricket Club, the A404 parking area and the Chorleywood House Estate, and there are toilets, baby changing and refreshments. [Details](https://www.chorleywood-pc.gov.uk/council_events/scary-skeleton-trail-29-october-10am/)
- **Spooky Story Boats**, Rickmansworth Waterways Trust, Rickmansworth (WD3 1JJ). Thursday 29, Friday 30 and Saturday 31 October 2026, hourly departures from 10am to 3pm. Children £12, adults £9; children must be with an adult, and one adult can bring more than one child. A 35-minute canal cruise with Halloween stories, followed by 30 minutes of crafts and games and a Halloween booklet to take home. Dressing up is welcome. Spaces are limited on each boat, so check there are enough tickets left for your whole group, adults included. [Details](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/rickmansworthwaterwaystrust/2429471)

## Shows and cinema

- **Disney Junior Film Club**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Sunday 25 October 2026, 11am and 3pm. Adults £4.50, children £3.50. An interactive hour of Disney Junior shorts with sing-alongs, dancing and games. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/disney-junior-film-club)
- **Hook**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Tuesday 27 October 2026, 2pm. £1 a ticket. A half-term screening of Spielberg's Peter Pan film (U, 142 minutes). [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/hook)
- **Dinosaur Adventure Live**, Watford Palace Theatre, Watford. Tuesday 27 October 2026, 2pm. Tickets from £21. A one-hour family show (no interval) with puppet dinosaurs, a raptor on the loose and a T-Rex finale; children are invited to stomp and roar along. [Details](https://watfordpalacetheatre.co.uk/events/dinosaur-adventure-live/)
- **Magic & Mayhem**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Wednesday 28 October 2026, 2pm. £15, recommended for ages 4+. A 60-minute family show of comedy magic and illusions from Kurtis and Rob. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/magic-mayhem)
- **The Utterly Revolting Science Show**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Thursday 29 October 2026, 11.30am and 3pm. Adults £19, children £17, family ticket £68; for ages 5 and up. A 90-minute show (with an interval) of gross-out science experiments and explosions. Watersmeet warns of loud bangs, stage fog and latex balloons, so it isn't suitable for anyone with a latex allergy. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/utterly-revolting-science-show)
- **Watford Film Club: Ghostbusters**, Watford Palace Theatre, Watford. Friday 30 October 2026, 8pm. Tickets from £7.50. A Halloween screening of the classic (PG, 1 hour 45 minutes), better suited to older children given the late start. [Details](https://watfordpalacetheatre.co.uk/events/watford-film-club-ghostbusters/)
- **Trick or Treat Show**, Pondside, The Parade, Watford. Saturday 31 October 2026, 5pm to 6pm, free. Halloween dance performances, a contortionist, street performers and, new this year, a free haunted house. Come in costume, and bring a carved pumpkin for the chance to win a prize. [Details](https://watfordtowncentre.com/event/watford-town-centre-trick-or-treat-show/)
- **Shaun the Sheep: The Beast of Mossy Bottom**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Sunday 1 November 2026, 11am and 3pm. Adults £4.50, children £3.50. Shaun's Halloween film (U), in which a mad-scientist experiment lets monsters loose around Mossingham. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/beast-mossy-bottom)

You can see everything Watersmeet has on over the holiday on its [October half term page](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/october-half-term).

## Other local events

- **Diwali: Festival of Light**, Croxley Green. Saturday 17 October 2026, 4pm to 7pm, free. A family-friendly parish council celebration with traditional and contemporary dance, music, food stalls and market traders. [Details](https://croxleygreen-pc.gov.uk/event/diwali-festival-of-light/)
- **Halloween Market Lates**, Watford Market, Watford. Friday 30 October 2026, 6pm to 10pm, free entry. A family-friendly evening of street food and live music, with free face painting from 6pm to 8pm, a roaming magician and fancy dress encouraged. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/news/article/1053/halloween-market-lates-30-oct-spooktacular-night-of-tricks-treats-and-scarily-good-food)
- **Diwali Celebrations**, Watford town centre. Sunday 1 November 2026, 11am to 7.30pm, free. Diya making and cultural performances during the day, then a Festival of Light parade and a rangoli lighting ceremony, across the Harlequin, the High Street, Pondside and Watford Palace Theatre. [Details](https://watfordtowncentre.com/event/diwali-celebration-2026/)

## Pumpkin picking

- **Spook-Fest**, Aldenham Country Park, Aldenham Road, Elstree (WD6 3BA). 10 to 31 October 2026, suitable for all ages. Your ticket covers the farm animals plus Halloween extras such as the Spooky Shack, a pumpkin hunt, giant puzzles, mini tractors, a bouncy castle and, on selected dates, scarecrow shows. Pumpkin picking and hay tractor rides are add-ons on certain dates. Book through the park's website. [Details](https://aldenhamcountrypark.co.uk/spookfest)
- **Pumpkin Festival**, Willows Activity Farm, Coursers Road, London Colney (AL4 0PF). Selected weekends and half term from 10 to 31 October 2026. Thousands of pumpkins to pick, with one free standard pumpkin for every paid child, plus a Halloween maze, pumpkin hunts, crafts, character shows, a Halloween disco and tractor rides. Included for members. [Details](https://www.willowsactivityfarm.com/events/pumpkin-festival/)
- **Pre-School Pumpkin Patch**, Willows Activity Farm, London Colney. Weekdays from 12 to 22 October 2026, 10.45am to 12.45pm. A quieter session for pre-schoolers with pumpkin picking (one free pumpkin per paid child), tractor rides, funfair rides and Peter Rabbit and friends. [Details](https://www.willowsactivityfarm.com/events/preschool-pumpkin-festival/)
- **Pumpkin Festival**, Odds Farm Park, Wooburn Common, near High Wycombe (HP10 0LX). Every Saturday and Sunday in October, and daily over half term from 24 October to 1 November 2026. Every paid child takes home a free pumpkin from the patch, and there are carving stations alongside the usual animals and play areas. The Pumpkin Family Nights have sold out. Booking online is cheaper. [Details](https://www.oddsfarm.co.uk/pumpkin-festival/)

## Days out

- **Dark Arts**, Warner Bros. Studio Tour London, Leavesden. Until 8 November 2026. Over 100 floating pumpkins in the Great Hall, Death Eaters with lighting and pyrotechnic effects, Dementors in the Forbidden Forest and duelling lessons in the Defence Against the Dark Arts classroom. New for 2026 are sound-effects demonstrations and Nagini creature displays. Tickets must be booked in advance, and you can ask staff if you'd like to bypass any of the areas. [Details](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/the-experience/whats-on/dark-arts/) · [Our listing](/activities/warner-bros-studio-tour-london/)
- **Terrific Tuesday**, Chiltern Open Air Museum, Chalfont St Giles. Tuesday 27 October 2026, 11am to 3.30pm. Tudor-themed family crafts, clay modelling and activities, all included in admission (it's a premium admission day; members go free). [Details](https://www.coam.org.uk/terrific-tuesdays)
- **Halloween Spectacular**, Chiltern Open Air Museum, Newland Park, Gorelands Lane, Chalfont St Giles (HP8 4AB). Friday 30 October 2026, 5pm to 9pm, with entry at 5pm or 6pm. £16.50 (members £8.50); under-16s must be with an adult. Fire performances, storytelling, trick or treating in the historic buildings, a bonfire with marshmallows, and a choice of a family-friendly woodland walk or a scary one for over-12s. Bring a torch and dress warmly. [Details](https://www.coam.org.uk/halloween)

## Good to know

- Hertfordshire Libraries says Zoolab is coming back to its libraries over October half term with Halloween animal sessions, but the dates and branches weren't visible to us. Check the [library events page](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/events-and-things-to-do/library-events.aspx) for your nearest branch.
- The shows, story boats and timed trails have limited spaces, so book ahead if you can. The town centre trail and Trick or Treat Show in Watford are free and you don't need to book.
- Woodland trails and pumpkin fields get muddy in late October, so wellies are a good idea.
