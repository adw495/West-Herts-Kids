Hertfordshire schools break up for **October half term on Friday 23 October**, and most children are off from **Monday 26 to Friday 30 October 2026**. (Check your own school's calendar, as academies and independent schools can differ.)

Here's what's running locally that week. We'll add dates as providers publish them.

## Sport and active camps

- **[Rickmansworth Gymnastics Club](/activities/rickmansworth-gymnastics-club/)** runs holiday camps for 4–14s at The Reach, including badge testing and a Halloween party on Thursday 29 October.
- **[Watford Gymnastics Club](/activities/watford-gymnastics-club/)** runs half-term camps across Watford, Croxley and South Oxhey.
- **[We Make Footballers Watford](/activities/we-make-footballers-watford/)** runs football camps for 4–12s of all abilities. New dates are added each term.
- **[Xtra Time Watford](/activities/xtra-time-watford/)** runs multi-activity camps for children aged 4 and up.

## Creative and STEM

- **[art-K Croxley Green](/activities/art-k-croxley-green/)** has half-term art workshops for ages 5–18 on Watford Road.
- **[Rise Studios](/activities/rise-studios-rickmansworth/)** runs dance and drama half-term workshops.
- **[Perform](/activities/perform-rickmansworth/)** has autumn half-term holiday courses in drama, dance and singing.
- **[Code Ninjas Watford](/activities/code-ninjas-watford/)** runs coding, robotics and AI camps for 5–14s.

## Free and low-cost ideas

- A walk round the **[Rickmansworth Aquadrome](/activities/rickmansworth-aquadrome/)** nature reserve (wellies advised).
- **[Cassiobury Park](/activities/cassiobury-park/)** playgrounds and the **[miniature railway](/activities/watford-miniature-railway/)** (£2 a ride, under-2s free).
