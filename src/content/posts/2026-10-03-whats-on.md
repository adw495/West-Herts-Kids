Autumn's properly here, and there's plenty going on for local families over the next nine days, from a charity dog show in Croxley to a fossil-hunting walk round the Aquadrome. We've also rounded up the half-term shows and Halloween trails that are worth booking now, before they sell out.

## This weekend (3–4 October)

- **Annual Charity Dog Show**, Stone's Orchard, Croxley Green. Sunday 4 October, 11am–4pm (Best in Show at 3:30pm). £1 donation per class. As well as the dog classes there's a Cuddly Canine Class where children parade their toy dogs, plus a bouncy castle, sand art and food stalls. [Details](https://croxleygreen-pc.gov.uk/event/annual-charity-dog-show/)

## During the week

- **Dark Arts at Warner Bros. Studio Tour London** ([listing](/activities/warner-bros-studio-tour-london/)), Leavesden, Watford. Runs until Sunday 8 November. The Studio Tour gets its Hallowe'en makeover, with floating pumpkins in the Great Hall, roaming Death Eaters, Dementors in the Forbidden Forest and wand-duelling lessons. It includes lighting, sound and pyrotechnic effects, so it may be a bit much for very young or sensitive children. [Details](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/whats-on/dark-arts)
- **Three Rivers Museum of Local History**, Basing House, High Street, Rickmansworth. Open Wednesday to Friday 2pm–4pm and Saturday 10am–2pm. Free, but donations are welcome. It's a small, friendly museum about how people lived and worked locally over the years, and its current exhibition is Franklins Water. [Details](https://trmt.org.uk/)

## Next weekend (10–11 October)

- **Chalk Tales and Fossil Trails** at Rickmansworth Aquadrome ([listing](/activities/rickmansworth-aquadrome/)), Frogmoor Lane, Rickmansworth. Saturday 10 October, 10:30am–12pm. Free, but you need to book. Fossil experts lead a guided walk about the Aquadrome's chalk landscape and the River Colne. Children are welcome but must be booked as a separate person and stay with an adult. A separate microfossil microscope workshop runs afterwards (1pm–3pm) at Batchworth Canal Centre and needs its own booking. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/news/chalk-stream-fossils-workshop)
- **Cassiobury junior parkrun** in Cassiobury Park ([listing](/activities/cassiobury-park/)), Watford. Sunday 11 October, 9am. Free. A weekly 2k run for 4 to 14-year-olds. It's cancelled on Sunday 4 October because the venue isn't available, so check the page before you go. [Details](https://www.parkrun.org.uk/cassiobury-juniors/)

## Coming up (book now)

- **Trick or Treat Trail**, Watford High Street and The Parade, Watford. Monday 26 to Friday 30 October, 10am–4pm. Free. Hunt for 10 ghosts hidden in shop windows over half term, and send in selfies for a chance to win a £100 Watford Gift Card. [Details](https://watfordtowncentre.com/event/trick-or-treat-trail-2026/)
- **Dinosaur Adventure Live**, Watford Palace Theatre, Watford. Tuesday 27 October, 2pm. From £21. An interactive hour-long show with puppet dinosaurs, baby dinos to feed and a T-Rex finale. [Details](https://watfordpalacetheatre.co.uk/events/dinosaur-adventure-live/)
- **Hook (U)**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Tuesday 27 October, 2pm. £1. A big-screen showing of the Spielberg family classic to warm up for Watersmeet's Peter Pan panto in December. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/hook)
- **Magic & Mayhem**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Wednesday 28 October, 2pm. £15. Comedy magic duo Kurtis & Rob put on an hour of illusions and slapstick, recommended for ages 4 and up. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/magic-mayhem)
- **Halloween Fun!**, Reveley Lodge, Elstree Road, Bushey Heath. Wednesday 28 and Thursday 29 October, with morning and afternoon sessions. Children £6, adults £3. Expect a Halloween trail, garden games, crafts, spooky story time and lots of ghoulish surprises around the grounds. Glitter tattoos cost a little extra. [Details](https://www.reveleylodge.org/events/)
- **The Utterly Revolting Science Show**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Thursday 29 October, 11:30am and 3pm. Adults £19, children £17, family ticket £68. Stefan Gates's Rude Science team bring burp machines, edible chemistry and loud bangs, recommended for ages 5 and up. It isn't suitable for anyone with a latex allergy. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/utterly-revolting-science-show)
- **Spooky Trail**, Chorleywood Common (near Cafe on the Common), Chorleywood. Thursday 29 and Friday 30 October, with hourly slots from 10am to 2pm. £3.50 per child plus a £1 admin fee. Follow skeletons and solve clues among the trees on a walk of about an hour. It's buggy-friendly, but expect mud. [Details](https://www.chorleywood-pc.gov.uk/council_events/scary-skeleton-trail-29-october-10am/)
- **Spooky Story Boats**, Rickmansworth Waterways Trust, Rickmansworth. Thursday 29 to Saturday 31 October, hourly from 10am to 3pm. Children £12, adults £9. A 35-minute narrowboat cruise with Halloween stories, followed by 30 minutes of Halloween crafts and games. [Details](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/rickmansworthwaterwaystrust/2429471)
- **Trick or Treat Show**, Pondside, The Parade, Watford. Saturday 31 October, 5pm–6pm. Free. Free Halloween street performances, a haunted house to creep through, and prizes for the best costumes and carved pumpkins. [Details](https://watfordtowncentre.com/event/watford-town-centre-trick-or-treat-show/)
