## Leisure centre lessons (Everyone Active)

All four leisure centres run Everyone Active's children's programme, starting with **Adult & Child** classes and moving through numbered stages. They run all year, which suits families who don't want a break in the holidays, but popular after-school slots fill quickly.

- **[William Penn](/activities/william-penn-leisure-centre/)** (Mill End, Rickmansworth) and **[South Oxhey](/activities/south-oxhey-leisure-centre/)** are the cheapest at **£37.29 a month**.
- **[Watford Central](/activities/watford-leisure-centre-central/)** and **[Woodside](/activities/watford-leisure-centre-woodside/)** (Garston) cost **£48.96 a month**. Both have two pools.

## Swim schools in Watford

- **[Aqua Swim School](/activities/aqua-swim-school-watford/)** has taught in Watford since 1990, at Watford Grammar School for Girls. Beginners start from 3½, lessons run Monday to Friday from 4.15pm and on Saturday mornings, and it costs **£143 for an 11-week term**. Parents wait outside rather than poolside.
- **[Swim4Life](/activities/swim4life-watford/)** is a family-run school with small classes from age one: a **Saturday Parent + Toddler class** at Watford Girls (11.30am), stages 1–7 after school at Cassio Metro Leisure Centre on Wednesdays and Thursdays, and one-to-one and two-to-one lessons on Tuesdays.
- **[Watford Swim School](/activities/watford-swim-school/)** teaches in a small private pool at Stud Green, Garston, with parents poolside. A 30-minute lesson is **£35 one-to-one** (£37 after 3.30pm and at weekends), **from £20 two-to-one**, and **£17 for Parent & Baby**. Its £16 four-to-one lessons run at Abbot's Hill School in Hemel Hempstead.

## Swimming lessons in Rickmansworth and Croxley Green

- **[William Penn Leisure Centre](/activities/william-penn-leisure-centre/)** in Mill End is the main leisure centre option (see above).
- **[Active Swim School](/activities/active-swim-school-rickmansworth/)** teaches at **Rickmansworth School** (Scots Hill, Croxley Green), **Royal Masonic School for Girls** and **York House School** (weekends). It covers babies, children and adults, with groups of up to four for beginners, one-to-one lessons and **tailored lessons for children with SEN**. Prices aren't published; it offers a free trial.

## Baby and toddler swimming

- **[Water Babies](/activities/water-babies-bushey-meadow-wood/)** in Bushey teaches from newborn in 30-minute weekly classes, with you in the water.
- **[Watford Swim School](/activities/watford-swim-school/)** takes babies from 4 months (Parent & Baby, term-time weekdays).
- **[Active Swim](/activities/active-swim-school-rickmansworth/)** and **[SwimFitz](/activities/swimfitz-home-swimming-lessons/)** start from 6 months; **[Swim4Life](/activities/swim4life-watford/)** from age one.
- The leisure centres' **Adult & Child** stage is the cheapest way to start.

## Holiday crash courses

- **[Watford Swim School](/activities/watford-swim-school/):** five consecutive days, 30 minutes a day. **£122.50** two-to-one or **£185** one-to-one per child.
- **[Active Swim School](/activities/active-swim-school-rickmansworth/):** holiday crash courses at Rickmansworth School; October half-term dates were "TBA" when we checked.
- In the summer holidays, Watford and Three Rivers councils have run **free swims for under-19s** at the leisure centres (27 July to 27 August in 2026). See the [Watford](https://www.watford.gov.uk/news/article/1031/free-gym-and-swim-returns-to-watford-this-summer) and [Three Rivers](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/sport/sport-children-and-young-people/free-swim-under-19) pages.

## One-to-one lessons at home

If you have access to a private pool, **[SwimFitz](/activities/swimfitz-home-swimming-lessons/)** coaches one-to-one across Rickmansworth, Chorleywood, Croxley and Watford, from 6 months. Lessons are 60 minutes, with no contract.

## Which should you choose?

- **Cheapest, all year:** William Penn or South Oxhey at £37.29 a month.
- **Small groups and term-time only:** Aqua Swim School or Swim4Life.
- **Nervous swimmers or SEN:** one-to-one at Watford Swim School, Active Swim's SEN lessons, or SwimFitz.
- **Need to catch up fast:** a holiday crash course.
- **Under-ones:** Water Babies or Watford Swim School's Parent & Baby.
