Waiting lists for children's swimming lessons can be long, so it's worth knowing all the local options. There are three main routes.

## 1. Leisure centre lessons

**[William Penn Leisure Centre](/activities/william-penn-leisure-centre/)** in Mill End and **[Watford Leisure Centre Central](/activities/watford-leisure-centre-central/)** both run Everyone Active's children's swimming programme for all abilities. They're usually the most affordable option and run all year, but popular slots fill quickly.

## 2. Independent swim schools

- **[Aqua Swim School](/activities/aqua-swim-school-watford/)** has taught in Watford since 1990, at Watford Grammar School for Girls. Beginners start from 3½, there are lessons Monday to Friday from 4:15pm and on Saturday mornings, and it costs £143 for an 11-week term.
- **[Watford Swim School](/activities/watford-swim-school/)** offers private lessons for babies and children, with weekly term-time lessons and holiday crash courses.

Independent schools tend to have smaller groups and follow school terms.

## 3. One-to-one lessons at home

If you have access to a private pool, **[SwimFitz](/activities/swimfitz-home-swimming-lessons/)** coaches one-to-one across Rickmansworth, Chorleywood, Croxley and Watford. Babies start from 6 months, and structured lessons from age 3.

## Which should you choose?

- **Complete beginners under 5:** a small-group swim school or parent-and-baby sessions build water confidence fastest.
- **School-age children who need to catch up:** holiday crash courses give a quick boost.
- **Nervous swimmers:** one-to-one lessons remove the pressure of a group.
