The county's term dates are only a starting point. Secondary schools in Watford, Rickmansworth and Three Rivers add their own INSET days, and several academies set different end-of-year dates altogether. Here's how nine local schools compare for 2026/27, taken from each school's own term-dates page or PDF.

**Hertfordshire County Council, for comparison:** term starts Tuesday 1 September 2026; October half term 26–30 October; back after Christmas Monday 4 January 2027; last day of the summer term Thursday 22 July 2027. Full county dates are in our [Hertfordshire term dates guide](/whats-on/herts-school-term-dates-2026-27/).

## What's different this year

- **Earlier summer finish.** Watford Grammar School for Boys, Watford Grammar School for Girls, Parmiter's and Queens' all finish on **Friday 16 July 2027**, nearly a week before the county's last day (Thursday 22 July). St Clement Danes, Croxley Danes and Westfield finish on **Wednesday 21 July**.
- **A longer October break.** Future Academies Watford is off from Friday 23 October (INSET) until **Monday 9 November**, two weeks rather than one.
- **Extra days around half term.** Watford Grammar School for Boys is off on Friday 23 October, Parmiter's from Thursday 22 October, and Kings Langley School has an INSET day on 23 October too.
- **A late January return.** Five of the nine schools have an INSET day on Monday 4 January and go back on Tuesday 5 January.
- **Late November closures.** St Clement Danes and Croxley Danes have an occasional day and day in lieu on 23–24 November; Westfield is closed on 26–27 November and Watford Grammar School for Girls on 27 November.
