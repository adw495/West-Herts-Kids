*Groups change times and take breaks in the holidays, so check the link before you go.*

Getting out of the house with a baby or toddler is easier when you know where to go. This is a weekday timetable of drop-in baby groups, rhyme times and stay-and-play sessions run by the Hertfordshire Family Centre Service, plus church toddler groups whose own websites list their times. Most cost between nothing and £2.50.

The Family Centre sessions below are from the Service's autumn timetables (September to December 2026). "Little Herts" sessions are drop-in with no booking, and the price is per family (up to four people). [All family centre timetables](https://www.hertsfamilycentres.org/events/whats-on-at-family-centres-in-hertfordshire.aspx)

## Monday

- **Baby Group (pre-walkers)**, The Reddings Family Centre, Bushey. A Family Centre drop-in for families with young babies, with time to play and talk to the family support team. Weekly, 1:30pm to 3pm (not on 28 December). £1.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca/sept-dec)

## Tuesday

- **Baby Group (pre-walkers)**, Westfield & Littlebury Best Start Family Hub, Croxley View, Watford. Weekly, 10am to 11:30am. £1.50 per family, drop in. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **Active Rhyme Time**, 336 Park Avenue (attached to Hartspring Community Centre), Bushey. Active rhymes and songs for 0 to 5-year-olds, siblings welcome. Weekly, 10am to 11am (not on 29 December). £2.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **Peep Learning Through Play**, Mill End Community Centre, Rickmansworth. The Family Centre's stay-and-play session for 0 to 5-year-olds (it used to be called Stay and Play), with a separate area for babies. Weekly, 10am to 11:30am (not on 27 October, 22 or 29 December). £2.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **Toddlers at North Bushey Baptist Church**, 218 Park Avenue, Bushey. A drop-in group for babies and pre-schoolers with their parents, grandparents or childminders, in the church hall with a fenced-off area in the church. Tuesdays, 9:30am to 12 noon, term time. Free, with a £1 donation asked to cover costs. [Details](https://northbusheybaptist.org.uk/whats-on/)
- **Active Rhyme Time**, Meriden Community Centre, Watford. For 0 to 5-year-olds. Weekly, 1:30pm to 2:30pm (not on 22 or 29 December). £2.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **Physical Fun**, OneYMCA Woodland Hub, Leavesden Country Park, Abbots Langley. An active play session for children from 18 months, siblings welcome. Weekly, 1:45pm to 2:45pm (not on 22 or 29 December). £2.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)

## Wednesday

- **Baby Group (pre-walkers)**, Beechfield Family Centre, Watford. Weekly, 10am to 11:30am (not on 21 October). £1.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **Peep Learning Through Play**, Westfield & Littlebury Best Start Family Hub, Croxley View, Watford. For 0 to 5-year-olds. Weekly, 10am to 11:30am (not on 28 October, 23 or 30 December). £2.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **St Peter's Tots**, St Peter's Church Hall, Bushey Mill Lane, Watford. Wednesday and Thursday mornings in term time, 9:30am to 11am. Because of space, the church says it can only welcome childminders from WD24 and the Meriden. [Details](https://www.stpeterswatford.org.uk/children-youth-families)
- **Toddlers at Bushey Baptist Church**, Chalk Hill, Bushey. Games, arts and crafts, songs, play equipment, bikes and tunnels. No need to book. Wednesdays, 1:30pm to 3pm, term time. [Details](https://www.busheybaptist.org.uk/Groups/395970/Toddlers.aspx)

## Thursday

- **St Luke's Toddlers**, St Luke's church hall, Langley Way (Cassiobury Estate), Watford. For children from birth to school age and their parent or carer, with free play, a themed story corner, singing at the end and free tea and coffee. Thursdays, 9:30am to 11am. A donation of £1 per adult and 50p per child is asked, but the church says not to worry if you can't pay every week. [Details](https://www.stlukeswatford.org/toddlers/)
- **St Peter's Tots**, St Peter's Church Hall, Bushey Mill Lane, Watford. Thursdays as well as Wednesdays, 9:30am to 11am, term time. [Details](https://www.stpeterswatford.org.uk/children-youth-families)
- **Baby Group (pre-walkers)**, Leavesden Family Centre, Watford. Weekly, 10am to 11:30am (not on 22 October). £1.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **SEND Chat & Play**, St Lawrence Church, High Street, Abbots Langley. A relaxed drop-in for families of children with special educational needs or disabilities, whether or not your child has a diagnosis. Weekly, 10am to 11am (a SEND Family Fun session runs on 29 October instead; not on 24 or 31 December). Free. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **Physical Fun**, Beechfield Family Centre, Watford. For children from 18 months. Weekly, 2pm to 3pm (not on 22 October, 24 or 31 December). £2.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)

## Friday

- **Active Rhyme Time**, Otley Way Family Centre, South Oxhey. Songs and rhymes for 0 to 5-year-olds. Weekly, 10am to 11am (not on 25 December or 1 January). £2.50 per family. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- **SEND Chat and Play**, Westfield & Littlebury Best Start Family Hub, Croxley View, Watford. Weekly, 10am to 11am (SEND Family Fun on 30 October; not on 25 December or 1 January). Free. On 20 November, professionals from the Early Years SEND team, Public Health Nursing team and Family Support Service will be there. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)

## Weekends

- **Saturdads**, Westfield & Littlebury Best Start Family Hub, Croxley View, Watford. A play session for children aged 0 to 8 and their dads or male carers. Saturday 17 October and Saturday 21 November, 10am to 11:30am. Free, drop in. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)

## Library rhyme times

- **Hertfordshire Libraries: Baby Rhyme Time and Storytime**, Watford, Rickmansworth, Croxley Green, Abbots Langley, Bushey, Oxhey and other branches. Baby Rhyme Time is a free half-hour of songs and rhymes for babies and toddlers, and Storytime is a free story session for pre-schoolers (donations welcome). Some libraries use Eventbrite for Rhyme Time and others don't need booking, so check your branch's listing for the day and time before you go. [Details](https://www.hertfordshire.gov.uk/services/libraries-and-archives/events-and-things-to-do/library-events.aspx)

## Good to know

- Family Centre drop-ins have limited spaces on a first come, first served basis, so it helps to arrive on time. If you bring more than four people, the Service asks for £1 for each extra person. [Details](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/sept-dec)
- Many more independent toddler groups run in church halls and community centres, including in Abbots Langley and Kings Langley. The Family Centre Service keeps lists of them for [Watford and Three Rivers](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca/parenttoddlerwatfordthreerivers) and for [Hertsmere, including Bushey](https://mailchi.mp/oneymca.org/hertsmere), though it points out that these groups run independently.
- The Family Centre Service also runs free Well Baby Clinics, breastfeeding support and bookable courses such as baby massage. Call 0300 123 7572 (Monday to Friday, 8am to 6pm) or see [its website](https://www.hertsfamilycentres.org/family-centres.aspx).
