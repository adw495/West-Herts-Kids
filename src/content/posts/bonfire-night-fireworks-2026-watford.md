Bonfire Night falls on Thursday 5 November in 2026, so most displays around Watford are on the weekend either side. Below are the family fireworks displays we've confirmed for 2026 on the organisers' or councils' own websites, nearest first. Times and prices can change, so check the link before you set off.

## Watford and Three Rivers

- **Cassiobury Park Fireworks**, Cassiobury Park, Watford. **Saturday 7 November 2026.** Entertainment from 6pm with Vibe 107.6 and local band The Nevers, then fireworks from 7.30pm. **Free**, organised by Watford Borough Council, with optional donations to collectors (cash or contactless) or online by QR code at the entrances. Food, drink and rides are paid for on site, and the council advises bringing cash. There's no general parking in the Gade Avenue car park from 3pm, apart from a limited number of Blue Badge spaces (first come, first served), and parking restrictions apply on roads around the park from 7am to 11pm, so use a town centre car park or public transport. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/news/article/1054/hertfordshire-s-largest-free-fireworks-display-returns-to-cassiobury-park)
- **Abbots Langley Fireworks Display**, Manor House Playing Fields, Abbots Langley (WD5 0DD). **Friday 6 November 2026.** Run by Watford Lions Club with support from Warner Bros. Studios Leavesden and Abbots Langley Parish Council: a professional display set to music, hot food and children's amusements, and the Manor House bar is open. No ticket price is given; donations are welcome on the night or online. Sparklers and personal fireworks are restricted in the grounds. Start times aren't published yet. [Details](https://www.abbotslangley-pc.gov.uk/council_events/abbots-langley-fireworks-display/)
- **Watford Legends Fireworks Night**, Metropolitan Bushey, Aldenham Road, Bushey (WD23 2TR). **Sunday 1 November 2026.** Gates 3pm, fireworks 6pm, closes 8pm. Pre-sale tickets £8.50 single or £30 for a family or group of four; on the day £10 single (online or at the gate). Under-2s free. There's also a kids' magic show, a funfair, live music and street food. No dogs, and no outside food or drink. There's no parking on site (including Blue Badge) or on Aldenham Road; Costco's car park has limited free parking for up to 90 minutes until 7pm. There's an accessible standing viewing area away from the main crowds. [Details](https://www.metropolitanbushey.co.uk/events/fireworks-night-2026/)

## A little further

- **St Albans Fireworks Spectacular**, Verulamium Park, St Albans. **Saturday 7 November 2026.** Gates 6pm, fireworks 7.30pm, finishes about 8.15pm. Online: adults £12.50, children (4–16) £7.50, family (2 adults, 2 children) £35. On the gate: adults £15, children £10, family £40. Children 3 and under free. The accessible area is reached through Gate 1 only. [Details](https://www.stalbanscathedral.org/event/st-albans-fireworks-spectacular)
- **Low Bang Fireworks at Willows Activity Farm**, near London Colney. **Friday 6 and Saturday 7 November.** Gates 6pm, fireworks 7.30pm. The farm describes it as "low bang but not no bang", so it may suit younger or noise-sensitive children. Spaces are limited and you need to book in advance; the farm is cashless. [Details](https://willowsactivityfarm.com/events/low-bang-fireworks)
- **Berkhamsted Fireworks**, Berkhamsted Cricket Club. **Saturday 7 November 2026.** Gates 5.30pm, display 7pm. Tickets are sold online through Berkhamsted Rotary. [Details](https://www.berkhamsted-tc.gov.uk/council_events/berkhamsted-fireworks/)
