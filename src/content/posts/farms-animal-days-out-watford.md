## Kings Langley

- **Langleybury Children's Farm**, Langleybury Lane, Kings Langley. A volunteer-run charity farm where you can meet sheep, goats, pigs, cows, ducks, geese, chickens, rabbits, guinea pigs, aviary birds and three donkeys, and buy animal feed in the farm shop. There's a play area for under-5s and over-5s, a mini football pitch, a zip wire on the woodland nature trail and a picnic garden, plus toilets with baby change and an accessible cubicle. Visits are a morning (11am to 1.30pm) or afternoon (2.30pm to 5pm) session, booked online up to 30 days ahead, at £6.25 per person for everyone over 1; tickets are non-refundable. You can pay on arrival only if tickets are still available online, and the farm suggests messaging first. No dogs. The website shows daily opening in the summer holidays but doesn't list autumn dates, so check its booking calendar for sessions. [Details](https://www.langleyburychildrensfarm.org.uk/copy-of-about-1) · [Our listing](/activities/langleybury-childrens-farm/)

## Elstree and Chalfont St Giles

- **Aldenham Country Park farm**, Aldenham Road, Elstree. The farm says it's home to over 100 animals, including cows, pigs, sheep, goats, alpacas, donkeys, Shetland ponies, rabbits and guinea pigs. The Farm Pass (from £6.95 on the booking system) covers all-day farm access, feeding the animals, a bouncy castle, tractor play and a bear hunt, and small animal petting sessions can be added. The park opens daily from 9am; in early October last farm entry was 3.30pm on weekdays and 4pm at weekends, and the adventure playground opens at weekends. There's a café and a kiosk. The car park has a fixed daily charge of £4.50 on term-time weekdays and £5.50 at weekends and in the holidays. Dogs on leads are welcome in the park but not in farm areas or play areas. [Details](https://www.aldenhamcountrypark.co.uk/plan-your-visit) · [Opening times](https://www.aldenhamcountrypark.co.uk/plan-your-visit/times) · [Booking](https://aldenhamcountrypark.checkfront.com/reserve/)
- **Chiltern Open Air Museum**, Newland Park, Gorelands Lane, Chalfont St Giles. Not a farm park as such, but a 45-acre outdoor museum of more than 30 historic buildings with a working historic farm with sheep, goats and chickens. It's open Fridays to Mondays from 10am to 5pm, daily in Bucks school holidays (26 to 29 October this half-term), and closes at 4pm from 25 October. Pre-booked tickets are £10.50 for children aged 4 to 17 and £13.50 for adults, under-4s are free, and the museum advises booking ahead. There's a large car park and a café, and dogs on short leads are welcome. [Details](https://www.coam.org.uk/visit/)

## London Colney and Wooburn Common

- **Willows Activity Farm**, London Colney, St Albans (AL4 0PF). A big farm park with animal paddocks, pat-a-pet and animal shows, plus Peter Rabbit Adventure Playground, indoor soft play, trampolines, funfair rides, a tractor ride and adventure golf, all included in the day ticket. It's open daily from 9.30am with last entry at 4.30pm, except 25 to 27 December and 1 January. Day tickets are from £14.95 plus a £0.75 booking fee for children 90cm and over and adults; children under 90cm are free but still need a ticket with the booking fee. You can pay on arrival on normal days, but timeslots are needed for big events such as Pumpkin Festival. Parking is free, picnics are welcome, and there's Mrs Rabbit's Kitchen, the Burrow Cafe (weekends) and a kiosk. [Details](https://www.willowsactivityfarm.com/faqs/) · [Tickets](https://willowsactivityfarm.digitickets.co.uk/tickets)
- **Odds Farm Park**, Wooburn Common, High Wycombe (HP10 0LX). An all-day ticket includes the undercover animal barn, animal activities and demonstrations run by the livestock team, a large indoor adventure play barn and outdoor play with sand play. Mini golf is extra at £3.50. It's open daily 10am to 5.30pm (last entry 4.30pm), but closed on term-time Tuesdays from October to March (it opens on Tuesdays in October half-term) and on Christmas Day, Boxing Day and New Year's Day. Online tickets are from £12.95 for ages 2 and up, from £4.95 for 1-year-olds, and under-1s are free. You don't have to pre-book, but online is cheaper. Parking is free, and there's Marmalade's Tea Room, a coffee bar and ice cream parlour, and picnic areas. Only registered service dogs are allowed. [Details](https://www.oddsfarm.co.uk/all-you-need-to-know-about-your-visit/) · [Opening times](https://www.oddsfarm.co.uk/plan-your-visit/opening-times/) · [Tickets](https://tickets.oddsfarm.co.uk/)

## Halloween and Christmas 2026

- **Willows Activity Farm** has its Pumpkin Festival on 10, 11, 17 and 18 October and daily from 23 to 31 October, with a free pumpkin for each paid child, a pumpkin hunt, a Halloween maze and shows, and booking is required. There's also a Pre-School Pumpkin Patch on weekday mornings from 12 to 22 October, low-bang fireworks on 6 and 7 November, the Santa Spectacular in November and December, and Storytime with Santa on 2, 7, 9 and 14 December. [Pumpkin Festival](https://www.willowsactivityfarm.com/events/pumpkin-festival/) · [Santa Spectacular](https://www.willowsactivityfarm.com/events/santa-spectacular/)
- **Aldenham Country Park** runs Spook-Fest from 10 to 31 October, with the Aldenham Mausoleum and Spooky Shack, pumpkin picking (10 to 11 and 17 to 31 October) and a tractor ride on selected dates. Winter-Fest's Santa's Magical Grotto runs every weekend from 28 November to 20 December and daily from 21 to 24 December, and booking is required. [Spook-Fest](https://www.aldenhamcountrypark.co.uk/spookfest) · [Winter-Fest](https://www.aldenhamcountrypark.co.uk/christmas)
- **Odds Farm Park** sells Pumpkin Weekend and October half-term tickets through its general admission page. Its Christmas Cracker, with a one-to-one visit to Santa, runs on selected dates from 27 November to 24 December 2026, including dedicated SEN sessions. Standard tickets are from £35 and need booking. [Christmas Cracker](https://www.oddsfarm.co.uk/christmas-cracker/) · [Tickets](https://tickets.oddsfarm.co.uk/)
- **Chiltern Open Air Museum** opens for a Halloween Spectacular on 30 October and a Festive Weekend on 5 and 6 December. [Details](https://www.coam.org.uk/visit/)

## Good to know

- Langleybury and Aldenham both sell animal feed on site. Langleybury caps it each day so the animals aren't overfed.
- You can bring a picnic to Langleybury (picnic garden), Willows (outdoor and covered picnic benches) and Odds Farm Park (picnic spaces around the park).
