## Karate and mixed martial arts

- **Freestyle Martial Arts**, Westfield Academy, Tolpits Lane, Watford. A blend of karate, taekwondo, kickboxing and kung fu for ages 7 and up, with a beginners' class on Fridays from 6pm to 6:40pm. After a free taster, a beginner's month costs £40 including a uniform and belt, and there are family discounts. [Details](https://www.freestylemartialarts.com/watford) · [Our listing](/activities/freestyle-martial-arts-watford/)
- **ZKS Martial Arts**, Nascot Wood Junior School, Nascot Wood Road, Watford. Classes by school year: Mini Ninjas (Reception to Year 2), Junior Martial Arts (Years 3 to 6) and Teen Martial Arts (Years 7 to 11), plus Little Dragons for younger children. Membership starts at £35 a month for one class a week, and you can try up to two sessions first. The class timetable is on the club's website. [Details](https://www.zksmartialarts.co.uk/martial-arts-watford/) · [Our listing](/activities/zks-martial-arts-watford/)
- **Shogun Practical Karate**, Rickmansworth, South Oxhey, Bushey and Watford. Children's karate at Arnett Hills School, Rickmansworth on Mondays (ages 5–10 at 6pm, 11–17 at 7pm), All Saints Church, Gosforth Lane, South Oxhey on Tuesdays and Thursdays (5–10 at 5:45pm, 11–17 at 6:45pm), Bromet School, Oxhey Road, Watford on Wednesdays (all ages 5–17 at 7pm) and The Grange Academy, London Road, Bushey on Saturdays (5–10 at 9am, then a family class at 10am). You can book a free trial; prices aren't on the website. [Details](https://shogunpracticalkarate.com/karate-classes/karate-classes-in-herts-middlesex/) · [Our listing](/activities/shogun-practical-karate/)
- **Shotokan Karate JKA**, Watford Woodside Leisure Centre, Horseshoe Lane, Watford. A JKA Shotokan club whose Tiny Tigers class is for ages 6 to 12, with a junior class for older children. ## Taekwondo

- **Leon Taekwondo Academy**, Woodside Community Centre (Brow Club), The Brow, Watford. A kids' class (ages 5+) on Mondays from 6pm to 7pm. The Bronze plan is £40 a month for one class a week. [Details](https://leontaekwondo.co.uk/classes/) · [Our listing](/activities/leon-taekwondo-watford/)
- **Rusty Taekwondo**, Croxley Scout Hut, Watford Road, Croxley Green. A local club with junior classes on Mondays at 4pm and Fridays at 5pm, plus a teen session on Fridays at 6pm. Contact the club for a free session. It doesn't publish ages or prices online. [Details](https://www.rustytaekwondo.com/)
- **BJ Academy**, Community Centre, Manor House Grounds, High Street, Abbots Langley. A non-profit taekwondo academy that has been in Abbots Langley for more than 20 years, teaching from age 4, from beginners to black belts. The first session is free. Its website hasn't been updated for a while, so get in touch to confirm current class times. [Details](https://www.bjacademy.co.uk/)

## Jiu-jitsu and ju-jitsu

- **Watford Gracie Jiu-Jitsu Academy**, 1A Garnett Close, Watford. The Gracie BullySafe programme teaches children assertiveness and leverage-based techniques without punching or kicking. Tiny Titans (ages 3+) is on Saturdays at 2pm, Little Grapplers (5–7) on Mondays and Wednesdays at 5pm and Saturdays at 2:30pm, and Junior Grapplers (8–12) on Mondays and Wednesdays at 6pm, Fridays at 5pm, Saturdays at 3:30pm and Sundays at 11am. Children's membership is £43 a month for one class a week or £58 for two, with a 10-day free trial. [Details](https://www.watfordgjj.co.uk/timetable/) · [Our listing](/activities/watford-gracie-jiu-jitsu/)
- **Fudoshin Ju-Jitsu**, Bushey and South Oxhey. Ju-jitsu taught as practical self-defence, in infant (5–7), junior (8–12) and intermediate (13–16) classes. Locally, classes are at Queens' School, Bushey on Wednesdays from 6pm, Bushey Grove Leisure Centre on Thursdays from 5pm and the South Oxhey dojo on Gosforth Lane on Saturdays from 9am. Children's training is £40 a month plus a £40 annual membership, and there's a free trial lesson. [Details](https://www.fudoshin.co.uk/Classes) · [Our listing](/activities/fudoshin-ju-jitsu/)

## Choosing a club

- **Striking or grappling:** karate, taekwondo and kickboxing are mostly kicks, punches and blocks. Jiu-jitsu and judo focus on holds, throws and groundwork. Some clubs mix the two.
- **Total cost:** monthly fees are only part of it. Ask about joining fees, uniforms, licences and grading fees, which some clubs list separately.
- **Try before you commit:** almost every club here offers a free first session or trial.
