A quieter week before half term, with a Diwali celebration in Croxley Green and the first Halloween trails opening. Half-term shows are selling, so the "book now" list is the long one this week.

## This weekend

- **Diwali – Festival of Light**, Yorke Mead Primary School (Dulwich Way, WD3 3PX), Croxley Green. Saturday 17 October, 4pm–7pm, with a procession to the library for the light switch-on at about 6.50pm. Community celebration with Bollywood and classical Indian dance, lantern making, diya painting, henna and face painting. [Details](https://croxleygreen-pc.gov.uk/event/diwali-festival-of-light/)
- **Totally Spooking Out! Halloween trail**, Rickmansworth Aquadrome, Rickmansworth. Friday 16 October to Sunday 15 November, any time. Free. A 10-stop family trail: scan a QR code at each stop to try on a Halloween mask on your phone. Takes about 45 minutes. [Details](https://www.threerivers.gov.uk/services/leisure-parks-culture/play/totally-spooking-out-trail-rickmansworth-aquadrome)
- **Dark Arts**, Warner Bros. Studio Tour London, Leavesden. Until Sunday 8 November. Included with a Studio Tour ticket, booked in advance. The Harry Potter tour's Halloween makeover, with floating pumpkins in the Great Hall, roaming Death Eaters and duelling lessons. [Details](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/the-experience/whats-on/dark-arts/)

## During the week

- The **Aquadrome Halloween trail** and **Dark Arts** carry on all week. Herts schools break up for half term on Friday 23 October; see our [half-term holiday camps guide](/whats-on/october-half-term-2026-holiday-camps/) and [Halloween and half-term events](/whats-on/halloween-half-term-2026-watford/).

## Coming up (book now)

- **Disney Junior Cinema Club**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Sunday 25 October, 11am and 3pm. £4.50 adults, £3.50 children. An hour-long big-screen sing-along and dance session for pre-schoolers and their grown-ups. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/disney-junior-film-club)
- **Dinosaur Adventure Live**, Watford Palace Theatre, Watford. Tuesday 27 October, 2pm. From £21. Interactive one-hour family show with puppet dinosaurs. [Details](https://watfordpalacetheatre.co.uk/events/dinosaur-adventure-live/)
- **Hook**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Tuesday 27 October, 2pm. £1. Half-term film screening of Spielberg's Peter Pan adventure. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/hook)
- **Magic & Mayhem**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Wednesday 28 October, 2pm. £15. One-hour comedy magic show for ages 4+, with plenty of audience participation. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/magic-mayhem)
- **The Utterly Revolting Science Show**, Watersmeet, Rickmansworth. Thursday 29 October, 11.30am and 3pm. £19 adults, £17 children, £68 family. Loud, messy science for ages 5+. It uses latex balloons, so it isn't suitable for anyone with a latex allergy. [Details](https://www.watersmeet.co.uk/utterly-revolting-science-show)
- **Spooky Story Boats**, Rickmansworth Waterways Trust (WD3 1JJ), Rickmansworth. Thursday 29 to Saturday 31 October, hourly 10am–3pm. £12 per child, £9 per adult. A 35-minute narrowboat trip with Halloween stories, then about 30 minutes of crafts and games. [Details](https://www.tickettailor.com/events/rickmansworthwaterwaystrust/2429471)
- **Spooky Trail**, Chorleywood Common (starts near the Café on the Common, WD3 5SG), Chorleywood. Thursday 29 and Friday 30 October, timed slots 10am–2pm. £3.50 per child plus a £1 booking fee; adults free. Buggy-friendly woodland trail with spooky clues; fancy dress welcome. [Details](https://www.chorleywood-pc.gov.uk/council_events/spooky-trail-29-october-11am/)
- **Halloween Market Lates**, Watford Market, Watford. Friday 30 October, 6pm–10pm. Free entry. Evening market with street food, live music, a roaming magician and free face painting from 6pm to 8pm. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/news/article/1053/halloween-market-lates-30-oct-spooktacular-night-of-tricks-treats-and-scarily-good-food)
