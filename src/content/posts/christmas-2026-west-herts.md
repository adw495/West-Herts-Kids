Christmas in West Herts can be as big or as small as you like, from a proper panto with a Watford twist to a market on the green. Below are the family events we've confirmed for 2026 from the organisers' own websites, plus a few regulars that haven't announced their dates yet. We'll update the page as more dates come in, so check back before you plan.

## Pantos and Christmas shows

- **A Christmas Carol: The Pantomime**, Watford Palace Theatre, Watford. 29 November 2026 to 3 January 2027. For the whole family; tickets are a flat £20 until the end of October. Dickens' tale turned into a sing-along panto with a Watford twist. There are relaxed performances on Wednesday 9 and Sunday 13 December (2.30pm) and a captioned and audio-described performance on Sunday 27 December (2.30pm). [Details](https://watfordpalacetheatre.co.uk/events/a-christmas-carol-the-pantomime/)
- **The Snowman: With Live Orchestra**, Watford Colosseum, Watford. Sunday 13 December 2026, 12.30pm and 3.15pm. Recommended for ages 3+; children aged 1 to 3 need their own ticket and under-1s go free on a lap. The film plays on the big screen while a live orchestra performs the soundtrack, with a Shaun the Sheep Christmas short too. [Details](https://www.watfordcolosseum.co.uk/events/the-snowman)

## Markets, lights and local events

- **Croxley Green Christmas Market**, Stones Orchard, Croxley Green. Saturday 5 December 2026, 2.30pm to 6pm. Run by Croxley Green Parish Council, with market stalls, festive treats, local crafts and entertainment for all ages. [Details](https://croxleygreen-pc.gov.uk/event/christmas-market-3/)
- **Kings Langley Christmas Lights Festival**, High Street, Kings Langley. 21 November; the parish council says timings and other activities will be confirmed nearer the date. The parish council has confirmed the village lights are coming back for 2026, and this evening marks the start of the season. [Details](https://kingslangley-pc.gov.uk/event/christmas-lights-festival/)
- **Watford Santa Dash**, Cassiobury Park, Watford. Sunday 6 December 2026. 5K and 1K fun runs, all abilities welcome. Put on a Santa suit and run to raise money for Watford Mencap. [Details](https://www.atwevents.co.uk/e/watford-santa-dash-13907) · [Cassiobury Park listing](/activities/cassiobury-park/)

### Usually runs, but 2026 dates to be confirmed

- **Rickmansworth Winter Fair**, Rickmansworth town centre. The Rickmansworth Town Team says 2026 will be its 13th Winter Fair, but hasn't published the date yet. In past years it has been a family evening with a funfair, festive food and gift stalls, local performers and Father Christmas. [Details](https://www.rickmansworthtown.co.uk/)
- **Watford Town Centre Winterfest and Christmas lights switch-on**, The Parade and High Street, Watford. A free event funded by Watford Town Centre BID and held every year, but the 2026 date hasn't been announced. Last year it was on a Saturday in mid-November, with free funfair rides, a market, a parade with Santa on his sleigh and the lights switch-on. [Details](https://www.watford.gov.uk/news/article/878/watford-town-centre-christmas-lights-switched-on-at-spectacular-winterfest-event)
- **Croxley Green Christmas lights switch-on and carols**, Croxley Green Library forecourt, Barton Way, Croxley Green. A regular parish council evening of carols and lights in late November. The 2026 date hasn't been listed yet. [Details](https://croxleygreen-pc.gov.uk/events/)

## A little further afield

- **Hogwarts in the Snow**, Warner Bros. Studio Tour London, Leavesden. 14 November 2026 to 17 January 2027. The Great Hall, Diagon Alley and the Hogwarts castle model are decorated for Christmas, and new for 2026 there's a look at how festive sound effects are made. You must book in advance. A sold-out Family Dinner in the Great Hall (ages 8+) is also on Friday 4 December. [Details](https://www.wbstudiotour.co.uk/the-experience/whats-on/hogwarts-in-the-snow/) · [Studio Tour listing](/activities/warner-bros-studio-tour-london/)
- **Santa Spectacular and Storytime with Santa**, Willows Activity Farm, London Colney. Santa Spectacular runs on selected dates in November and December 2026. Storytime with Santa is on 2, 7, 9 and 14 December 2026. The Santa Spectacular includes a visit to Santa's grotto with a gift from his Toy Shop, plus farm shows and rides. Storytime is a group show in the indoor theatre, with no one-to-one Santa visits. You must book online. [Details](https://www.willowsactivityfarm.com/events/santa-spectacular/) · [Storytime](https://www.willowsactivityfarm.com/events/storytime-with-santa/)
- **Christmas Cracker**, Odds Farm Park, near Beaconsfield. Selected dates from 27 November to 24 December 2026. From £35 per child (members from £22). A full farm day with a one-to-one Santa visit, a cuddly bear to make, a gift from the toy shop and festive shows. Some dates are SEN-only sessions. [Details](https://www.oddsfarm.co.uk/christmas-cracker/)

## Good to know

- Book pantos and Santa visits early. The Snowman sells out every year, according to the Colosseum, and the Studio Tour's family dinner has already gone.
- Many free town events, like light switch-ons and fairs, only announce their dates a few weeks ahead. We'll add them here as they're confirmed.
- Look out for relaxed and SEN-friendly sessions, like the relaxed panto performances at the Palace and the SEN-only dates at Odds Farm.
